Name: Cultist of Obliviosa

Type: Humanoid
Size: Medium
Alignment: Lawful Evil
Armor Class: 13 (leather armor)
Hit Points: 33 (6d8 + 6)
Speed: 30 ft.

Abilities:

  - Strength: 10 (+0)
  - Dexterity: 12 (+1)
  - Constitution: 12 (+1)
  - Intelligence: 14 (+2)
  - Wisdom: 16 (+3)
  - Charisma: 14 (+2)

Skills: Arcana +4, Deception +4, Religion +4
Languages: Any one language (usually Common)
Challenge Rating: 2 (450 XP)

Actions:

  - Scimitar. Melee Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.
  - Dagger. Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +2 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.
  - Mind Wipe (1/day). The cultist casts a spell on one creature within 30 feet that it can see. The target must make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 15) or forget what it was doing and lose its next turn.

Equipment: Leather armor, scimitar, dagger, holy symbol, 10 gp.

Description: The Cultist of the Mind's Eye is a devoted follower of a mysterious deity of knowledge and secrets. They are often found in libraries or ancient ruins, searching for hidden lore and artifacts that can help their cult achieve its goals. They use their magic to manipulate the minds of others, erasing memories or planting false ones. In combat, they rely on their speed and cunning to strike from surprise and confuse their opponents. If cornered, they fight with a scimitar and a dagger, but they prefer to avoid direct confrontation whenever possible.
