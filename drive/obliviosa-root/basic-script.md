# Finale

Once the players have figured out Liv's mechanics, they can exploit her weaknesses, and challenge her to the final battle. They can either seek her out in the Forgotten Reach, or wait for her to come and feed in town. Either way, they will battle Liv and several Forgotten, depending on how tactful they are.
