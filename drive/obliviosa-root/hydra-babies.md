Name: Hydra-Spawn

Type: Aberration
Size: Medium
Alignment: Chaotic Evil
Armor Class: 14
Hit Points: 25
Speed: 30 ft., swim 30 ft.

Abilities:

Multiattack. The Hydra-Spawn can make two tentacle attacks.

Tentacle. Melee Weapon Attack: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) acid damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned for 1 minute. The creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Memory Drain. The Hydra-Spawn targets one creature it can see within 60 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 14 Intelligence saving throw or take 3d6 psychic damage and forget a random memory of the DM's choice. A creature that can't remember anything (e.g., has lost all of its memories) is immune to this effect.

Swallow. The Hydra-Spawn makes one tentacle attack against a Medium or smaller creature it is grappling. If the attack hits, the target is swallowed, and the grapple ends. The swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside the Hydra-Spawn, and it takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of the Hydra-Spawn's turns. The Hydra-Spawn can have only one creature swallowed at a time.

Skills: Stealth +5
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
Languages: Understands Common and Abyssal but can't speak
Challenge: 4 (1,100 XP)
