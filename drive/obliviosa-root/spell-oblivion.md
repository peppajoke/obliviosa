Spell Name: Oblivion

School: Necromancy

Level: 7th

Casting Time: 1 hour

Range: 100 yards

Components: V, S, M (a vial of black ink, a lock of hair from a loved one, and a pearl worth at least 500 gp)

Duration: Instantaneous

Description:

Veil of Forgotten Memories is a potent necromantic ritual designed to manipulate the memories of a group of individuals, causing them to forget specific events or persons. The caster inscribes cryptic runes with black ink on a designated surface and intertwines the lock of hair with the arcane symbols. The pearl serves as the focal point, channeling magical energy to fuel the spell.

Due to the unpredictable nature of this forbidden magic, a failed casting may result in chaotic memory manipulation. Memories may collide, intertwine, or dissipate entirely, creating confusion and unpredictable consequences. The unstable magical energies may even summon otherworldly beings to Earth, disrupting reality itself.

The use of this dark and forbidden magic comes at a great cost, and the aftermath of a botched casting may draw malevolent entities from beyond, seeking to exploit the weakened barriers between worlds.

The Veil of Forgotten Memories serves as a dangerous spell, one that can alter history and unleash unforeseen consequences upon the unwary. Its dark legacy stands as a cautionary tale, warning against tampering with the fragile tapestry of memories and the dangers of challenging the boundaries of forbidden magic.
