My Beloved son Thorne,

I write to you in desperation, for something is amiss here at the farmhouse. I fear that my memories are not my own, and that I cannot trust them anymore. I am haunted by a wailing song that echoes through the halls, and I cannot escape its piercing melody.

As I wander through the rooms, I find evidence of a life that I do not remember. There are toys and clothes that are not mine, and sketches of a family that I cannot recall. I do not remember if I had any other children, but I ask you now, do you remember any brothers or sisters?

I cannot remember how you were born, my son. Do you remember your mother? Was she kind to you? Did she love you?

I am lost without your guidance, for I no longer know what is real and what is not. I implore you to return to the farmhouse and help me understand the meaning of this wailing song. It creeps into my mind like a malevolent spirit, and I fear that it will drive me mad.

Please, my son, come home to me. Help me remember who I am and who we were. Help me find my way back to the light.

With love and desperation,

Your loving father, Ashe Blackwood
