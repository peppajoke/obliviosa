The town is too quiet. Something is off, I can feel it in my bones. But I can't put my finger on it. It's like everyone is just gone, disappeared without a trace. But I know that can't be right. I mean, I know everyone in this village. Don't I?

I woke up to find that my entire house was rearranged. All the furniture was moved, but I don't remember doing it myself. Did someone break in? But there's no sign of forced entry. I'm starting to feel like I'm losing my mind.

I was walking around today when I saw a house that looked familiar. I recognized it, but I can't remember why. It's like a piece of my memory is missing. I tried to shake it off and move on, but it's bothering me. I hope I can remember soon.

The town is still so quiet. I can't find anyone. It's like they've all vanished into thin air. But I can still hear something moving in the distance, something that's following me. I'm scared, but I don't know why.

Something strange is going on in this town. There are stores that weren't here yesterday, and they all seem to be abandoned. Nobody knows where they came from or who owned them. It's like they just popped up overnight. I can't help but feel like something is watching me.

I found a shack to the east of the village. I don't know how I got there, but it feels important. There's something inside, but I can't remember what it is. I think I lost a part of my memory there. I need to go back and investigate.

I discovered some new stores in town today, but they were all abandoned. No one knows where they came from. It's like they just appeared out of nowhere. Am I losing it? I'm going to go see a doctor tomorrow.

I keep having these strange dreams, where I'm being chased by something. I don't know what it is, but it's always just out of sight. I saw a blue monster eat up an entire family, except the father and sun. What the hell is that about?

I saw a woman today with blue hair. She's quite attractive, and there's something about her that feels off. I can't quite place it, but my gut tells me to be cautious.

Something funny happened today. A local bard was playing music in the square, and one of the stabled horses just screamed over his entire song. The guy was so mad, he got no tips. I feel bad but it was really funny. It really helps to laugh in these trying times.

I found a map of the village today, and there was a spot marked on it that I've never seen before. It's to the east of town, near the forest. I feel like I have to go there, like there's something I need to find.

I found the spot on the map, and there was a shack there. I went inside, but I don't remember anything…I was just…home after.

I've been thinking about it, and I think people are disappearing. But it's not just that they're gone, it's like they were never even there in the first place. Like they were erased from history. How else can you explain all the empty houses? Where is everyone?!

The mayor knows. I could feel it. He didn't want to hear what I had to say. I think he's in on it.

I don't know what's happening to me. Something is closing in, and I'm losing huge chunks of time. I can't remember most of the days anymore. The town's population keeps dwindling, and I don't know why. If you're reading this, you've probably found my journal. I've known all of you for a long time, and I hope you can figure out what's going on. Don't trust anyone, not even yourselves.

I went to visit Thaddeus Finn, a powerful mystic that some people say can solve any problem. He warned me that his solutions often backfire in unforeseen ways, as he believes it's the inevitable balancing of the universe.

Thaddeus shared with me some interesting theories about the monster and the town. He thinks that the monster may have taken our memories as a form of payment, and that restoring them may have unintended consequences. He suggested that some memories may be better left forgotten.

After hearing all of this, I'm unsure if I should ask for Thaddeus' help to restore my lost memories. It's tempting, but I fear that it may do more harm than good. I'll need to think on this more before making a decision.

I keep hearing a strange song in my head. It's like nothing I've ever heard before, but it's so beautiful…I need to focus.

I can't do this anymore. I don't want to disappear like the others. If I'm swallowed up by the world and forgotten by all, did I ever even exist? I love Lumenshire, but I love my life more. I'm sorry Lumenshire, I can't protect you anymore.
