# Museum of Wonders

# Heist

  - Museum items are protected with security magic. If any intruder is detected, a spell activates and teleports the items to an offsite vault. Marcus does not know where this vault is.
  - Each item requires a check to steal. When checks fail, the chance of being caught increases. If the players are caught, the items are teleported out of the museum, and the arcane construct guards (1d6/2 of them, rounded down) emerge to fight them.

# Big ticket Items

  - Vial of complete restoration
  - Staff of silence
  - Eternal Tome of Lumenshire

# Other items

Crystal Orb of Clairvoyance: A small crystal orb that, when activated, allows the user to scry on a location of their choice within a 1-mile radius. The scrying effect lasts for 10 minutes and provides visual and auditory information.

Chronomancer's Pocket Watch: A pocket watch with a mysterious, ornate design. Once per long rest, the user can activate the watch to cast the spell "Haste" on themselves, granting increased speed and an additional action for one minute.

Feyfire Lantern: This beautifully crafted lantern emits a soft, magical light that can illuminate an area up to 30 feet in radius. The light produced by the lantern is considered sunlight and can dispel magical darkness or affect creatures vulnerable to sunlight.

Mask of Disguise: A porcelain mask that grants the wearer the ability to cast the "Disguise Self" spell at will. The disguise created can mimic any humanoid appearance of the same size category.

Cloak of Elvenkind: This intricately woven cloak grants the attuned creature advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks. Additionally, they cannot be tracked by non-magical means unless they choose to leave a trail.

Harp of Serenity: When played skillfully, this magical harp produces calming melodies that soothe the minds and emotions of those who listen. Creatures within a 30-foot radius must make a Wisdom saving throw or become charmed and unable to take aggressive actions for 1 minute.

Orb of Screaming: When pressed firmly with five fingers, the orb dissipates and lets out a piercing scream for 1 minute before dissolving
