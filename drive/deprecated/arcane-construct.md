Name: Ironclad
Race: Arcane Construct
Alignment: Neutral

Ability Scores: STR 18, DEX 12, CON 16, INT 10, WIS 10, CHA 6
Hit Points: 60
Armor Class: 18

Attacks: Slam: +7 to hit, 1d8+4 bludgeoning damage

Features and Traits:

Construct Nature: You do not require air, food, drink, or sleep.
Magic Resistance: You have advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
Immutable Form: You are immune to disease, poison, and being charmed or exhausted.
Arcane Construct Immunities: You are immune to necrotic and psychic damage. You cannot be poisoned.

Equipment: Integrated Armor Plating: AC 18; Slam attack

Background Story: Ironclad is a powerful arcane construct created by a skilled artificer to serve as a guardian of ancient ruins. Its purpose is to protect valuable artifacts and ward off intruders. With its sturdy metal frame and enchanted armor plating, Ironclad is a formidable defender against any who would dare to disturb the sanctity of the ruins.

Note: As an arcane construct, Ironclad doesn't have traditional ability scores or proficiencies in the same way that humanoid characters do. Its strengths lie in its physical attributes, immunities, and resistances, making it a formidable force on the battlefield.
