Character Name: Liv

Physical Description: Once a small girl, Liv is now a twisted and monstrous abomination. She is the false hydra.

Liv, once an innocent and bright-eyed child, now embodies a chilling transformation that stands as a stark contrast to her former self. The monstrous hydra beast she has become is a horrifying sight to behold. Towering at an intimidating height of thirty feet, her colossal body is covered in shimmering blue scales, reflecting the light like a shimmering ocean wave. Sinewy tentacles, like dark serpents, writhe menacingly from her shoulders, undulating with a life of their own.

Her massive jaws, lined with razor-sharp teeth, gape open with an unholy hunger, a maw capable of consuming those unfortunate enough to cross her path. Her eyes, once filled with innocence, now gleam with an eerie azure glow, filled with the pain and torment of her tragic fate. The once loving child has been consumed by a dark force beyond her control, and now she is little more than a relentless force of destruction and oblivion.

Historical Description:

Liv, the daughter of Fawkes and Elara, was a radiant presence in Lumenshire before the catastrophic event that changed her life forever. A precocious child with a natural aptitude for magic, she was the light of her father's life and the delight of the townspeople. Her days were filled with laughter and curiosity, her mind constantly buzzing with questions about the world and its wonders.

However, her childhood was far from idyllic. Behind closed doors, she suffered under the cruel and unstable hand of her mother, Elara. The mental anguish of her mother's torment took a toll on her young mind, leaving emotional scars that would haunt her for years to come.

Liv was but nine years old when the tragic event occurred. Witnessing the violent altercation between her parents, she fled to the town for help, desperate to save her father from the consequences of his actions. Consumed by grief and the desire to protect her father, she sought to erase the memories of his alleged crime from everyone's minds, attempting a dark spell that would alter the course of her fate forever.

The spell, fueled by desperation and sorrow, went terribly wrong, resulting in a nightmarish transformation. Liv's body morphed into a terrifying blue hydra beast, her mind and soul forever entwined with a malevolent force that turned her into a relentless devourer of memories. The once-beloved daughter was now a fearsome creature, plagued by isolation and tormented by the guilt of her actions.

Her rampage through Lumenshire that night left behind a trail of destruction and despair, devouring those she encountered and erasing all traces of their existence from the memories of those who knew them. The once-vibrant town fell into a collective state of amnesia, as memories faded like fleeting dreams, leaving behind a legacy of loss and sorrow.

In the aftermath of that dreadful night, Liv retreated to the shadows, avoiding all contact with her former life. The townsfolk, unable to recall the true horrors of that night, now live in fear of The Forgotten, unaware that the very creature they fear is the embodiment of their once-beloved daughter and friend.

In her solitude, Liv struggles with the knowledge that her existence is a blight upon the town she once loved. In her heart, she longs for forgiveness and absolution, yearning to reclaim the innocence she once possessed. But bound by her monstrous form and cursed existence, she remains an elusive enigma, a reminder of the dark magic that haunts Lumenshire, and a daughter lost to the shadows of tragedy.

# Character sheet

Name: Liv (Hydra Form)
Race: Monstrous Hydra
Alignment: Chaotic Evil
Type: Monstrosity
Challenge Rating: 10 (5,900 XP)
Armor Class: 17 (Natural Armor)
Hit Points: 195 (17d12 + 85)
Speed: 40 ft., swim 40 ft.

Abilities: STR 23 (+6), DEX 12 (+1), CON 21 (+5), INT 2 (-4), WIS 10 (+0), CHA 7 (-2)
Saving Throws: DEX +5, CON +9
Skills: Perception +6
Damage Immunities: Poison
Condition Immunities: Poisoned
Senses: Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
Languages: Understands Common but can't speak

Multiattack: Liv can use her Frightful Presence. She then makes three attacks: one with her bite and two with her tentacles.

Bite: Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage.

Tentacles: Melee Weapon Attack: +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 16) if Liv isn't already grappling a creature, and the target takes 17 (2d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage at the start of each of Liv's turns. Liv can have up to three targets grappled at once.

Frightful Presence: Each creature of Liv's choice that is within 120 feet of Liv and aware of her must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature's saving throw is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to Liv's Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day): If Liv fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

Regeneration: Liv regains 10 hit points at the start of her turn. If she takes acid or fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of her next turn. Liv dies only if she starts her turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.

Limited Amphibiousness: Liv can breathe both air and water, but she needs to be submerged at least once every 4 hours to avoid suffocating.

Lair Actions: Liv's lair actions reflect the corrupted memories of her victims. For example, memories of peaceful moments may manifest as illusions of serenity, while memories of combat could manifest as spectral fighters. These lair actions are triggered at initiative count 20 and can be used three times between long rests:

Memory Eruption: Liv targets a creature within 60 feet that has had its memory altered by her Frightful Presence. The creature must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or take 3d6 psychic damage and have its memory restored.

Spectral Guardian: Liv summons a spectral figure to protect her. The figure has the same statistics as a Specter and acts on Liv's initiative count. It remains for 1 minute or until destroyed.

Distorted Reality: Liv reshapes reality around her, causing all creatures within 30 feet to make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, creatures perceive an altered version of reality for 1 minute, granting them disadvantage on attacks and ability checks.

Legendary Actions (3/Day): Liv can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Liv regains spent legendary actions at the start of her turn.

Attack: Liv makes one attack with her bite or one with her tentacles.
Move: Liv moves up to half her movement speed.
Frightful Presence (Costs 2 Actions): Liv uses her Frightful Presence.

Legendary Lair Actions (3/Day): On initiative count 20, Liv can take a legendary lair action to summon corrupted memories of her victims. These lair actions can be used three times between long rests:

Memory Maze: Liv warps the surroundings, creating an illusion of a twisted maze. All creatures within 30 feet must make a DC 16 Intelligence saving throw or become disoriented, granting them disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn.

Phantasmal Assault: Liv conjures spectral images of her victims to assault intruders. She can make one attack with her tentacles against a creature within 60 feet.

Reverie of Oblivion: Liv targets a creature within 60 feet, attempting to erase its memory of a specific event. The creature must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or lose the memory permanently, suffering from confusion for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
