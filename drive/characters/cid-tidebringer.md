Cid Tidebringer

Cid Tidebringer is a striking and charismatic figure, standing at an impressive 6 feet with a lean and toned physique. His rich, deep ebony skin is a testament to the countless days spent under the sun and the salt-laden winds of the sea. His short, curly black hair exudes an air of untamed adventure, and his eyes, the color of the midnight ocean, sparkle with a mix of wisdom and longing.

His attire is both practical and adorned with symbols of his love for the sea. He wears a weathered leather jacket, bearing patches and emblems from different ports, a tribute to the places he's explored and the memories he's collected. Around his neck hangs a pendant of a silver seagull, a talisman representing his dream of soaring through the skies one day.

Backstory:

Born and raised in a bustling coastal town, Cid Tidebringer's passion for the sea ignited at an early age. Growing up amid the bustling harbor and hearing the mesmerizing tales of sailors returning from their journeys, Cid found solace and freedom in the vast expanse of the ocean.

As a young boy, he spent hours watching the gulls glide effortlessly through the sky, yearning to join them in the realm of the clouds. The sea, with its vastness and unpredictability, beckoned to him like an enchantress, and he knew that his destiny lay beyond the horizon.

His family, seasoned sailors themselves, recognized Cid's innate connection with the ocean. Under the loving guidance of his father, a respected captain, and his nurturing mother, a gifted navigator, Cid learned the art of sailing and navigating the treacherous waters.

With the salty breeze in his hair and the thrill of adventure in his heart, Cid set sail on his first voyage alongside his parents. The thrill of charting new courses, discovering hidden coves, and encountering exotic cultures captivated his imagination. Each journey deepened his love for the sea and fueled his dream of someday soaring through the sky like the majestic gulls he admired.

As time passed, Cid's reputation as an exceptional sailor spread far and wide. Adventurers and travelers sought his expertise, and it was during one of these encounters that he crossed paths with the party. Intrigued by their quests and shared yearning for exploration, he offered his guidance and friendship, becoming an invaluable member of their group.

Though he chose not to wield weapons like his companions, Cid's navigational skills, unwavering loyalty, and affable nature brought a unique balance to the party dynamic. His dream of flying persisted, and he often found himself gazing longingly at the seagulls that circled above, secretly wondering what it would be like to dance with the clouds.

Throughout their many adventures, Cid's yearning for the skies grew stronger, driving him to learn about flying creatures, magical devices, and ancient legends that could grant him wings. As the party's journeys led them to remarkable places, Cid's heart remained steadfast, embracing the ever-changing tides of life while cherishing the hope of one day fulfilling his dream to fly among the heavens.
