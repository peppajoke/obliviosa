Seraphina Dawnbringer

# Known background

Seraphina Dawnbringer, a formidable and battle-hardened warrior, emerged as one of the first to take up arms alongside Fawkes in the face of the resurgent Forgotten threat. As the daughter of a renowned knight, she was raised with a strong sense of duty and honor. From a young age, Seraphina trained tirelessly with the sword, honing her combat skills and developing a fierce determination to protect her homeland, Lumenshire.

Her unwavering dedication caught Fawkes' attention, and he recognized in her a kindred spirit—someone willing to sacrifice everything for the greater good. With every battle fought, Seraphina's reputation as a skilled and fearless leader grew, and she soon earned the respect and admiration of her peers.

As the Luminous Vanguard was established, Seraphina's unmatched strength and strategic brilliance earned her the position of its commander, making her Fawkes' most trusted advisor and right-hand woman. Her loyalty to him and the safety of Lumenshire knew no bounds, and she stood unwaveringly at his side, leading their forces against the malevolent threat of the Forgotten.

# Secret history

Seraphine remembers fleeting glimpses of the truth. She has nightmares about the three headed monster, Liv. For whatever reason, she has not completely forgotten her wrath, and recalls the true events in her dreams.

Class: Paladin
Alignment: Lawful Good
Level: 10
Background: Luminous Vanguard

Proficiency Bonus: +4
Armor Class: 20 (Plate Armor, Shield)

Hit Dice: 1d10 per level
Speed: 30 ft.

Skills: Persuasion +8, Religion +4, Insight +6, Athletics +7

Features and Traits:

Divine Sense: Can detect celestial, fiend, or undead presence within 60 feet (1/LR).
Lay on Hands: Can heal up to 50 hit points per day, curing diseases or neutralizing poisons.

Improved Divine Smite: Divine Smite deals extra 1d8 radiant damage with each hit.

Equipment: Holy Avenger (Magical Greatsword); Plate Armor; Shield; Potion of Healing x2; Explorer's Pack; Holy Symbol of the Luminous Vanguard; Badge of honor representing membership in the Luminous Vanguard

Personality Traits:

Devoted and Righteous: Committed to upholding justice, protecting the innocent, and smiting evil wherever it may lurk.
Compassionate: Shows kindness and empathy, seeking to inspire hope in others and heal the wounded.
Focused: Maintains unwavering dedication to the cause of the Luminous Vanguard, always ready to face darkness head-on.

Backstory: Seraphina Dawnbringer is an aasimar paladin who answered the divine call to serve in the Luminous Vanguard, a sacred order devoted to defending the realms against darkness and malevolence. Blessed with celestial heritage, she radiates an aura of divine light that instills hope and strength in her comrades.

From a young age, Seraphina showed signs of a higher purpose, guided by visions and divine messages that led her to train under the tutelage of experienced paladins. She quickly rose through the ranks, earning her place as a respected and renowned member of the Luminous Vanguard.

With her Holy Avenger in hand, Seraphina faces evil without hesitation, her strikes empowered by radiant energy that purges darkness from the world. Her conviction in justice and righteousness fuels her every action, and her leadership on the battlefield inspires courage in those who stand beside her.

Now, as a seasoned member of the Luminous Vanguard, Seraphina travels the land, seeking out malevolent forces and confronting them with unwavering determination. Her unwavering faith in her divine mission and her selfless dedication to protecting the innocent make her a beacon of hope and a formidable force against the encroaching darkness.
