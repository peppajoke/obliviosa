Ashe Blackwood

Ashe Blackwood, a very old and paranoid man, lives a reclusive existence off the grid. His long, white beard cascades down his weathered face, a testament to the passage of time and the weight of his troubled mind. The burden of a tragic past weighs heavily on him, as he has lost his entire family to the monstrous entity known as Liv, though his memory has cruelly forsaken them.

His secluded home bears witness to the torment of his memories, filled with evidence of his lost loved ones, creating a labyrinth of paranoia and fear within the confines of its walls. Ashe's once sharp mind now battles the encroaching tendrils of madness, causing him to question his own sanity as he obsessively guards against any perceived threat.

Among the shadows of his home, attack dogs serve as his only companions and guardians. They mirror their master's mistrust, ever alert to the world beyond, their loyalty a reflection of the bond forged between man and beast in times of solitude.

In the depths of his solitude, Ashe grapples with the haunting absence of memories that haunt him, unable to reconcile the gaps in his past. As he navigates the twisted maze of his own mind, he becomes a tragic figure, lost within the shattered fragments of his past, a man clinging desperately to what remains of his sanity.

# Character sheet

Name: Ashe Blackwood
Race: Human
Class: Paranoid Hermit
Alignment: Neutral
Age: Very old (exact age unknown)
Background: Hermit

Ability Scores: STR 10 (+0), DEX 12 (+1), CON 8 (-1), INT 14 (+2), WIS 16 (+3), CHA 6 (-2)
Proficiency Bonus: +2
Armor Class: 12
Hit Points: 24 (4d8 - 4)
Hit Dice: 4d8
Speed: 30 ft.
Saving Throws: Wisdom +5, Intelligence +4
Skills: Perception +5, Investigation +4, Survival +5, Animal Handling +5

Features and Traits:

Darkvision: 60 feet
Hermit Background Feature: Silent Speech - Can convey simple ideas with body language and gestures to communicate with animals.
Paranoia: Ashe gains advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.
Sanity Wane: Due to his fragile mental state, Ashe has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws to resist madness-inducing effects.
Memories Lost: Ashe has forgotten most details about his family but retains fragments of evidence scattered throughout his home.

Equipment: Long white beard; Tattered cloak and worn clothing; Wooden staff for support and protection; Journal filled with cryptic notes and scribbled thoughts; Collection of peculiar trinkets, mementos of the past he cannot recall; Multiple attack dogs, fiercely loyal yet as paranoid as their master

Personality Traits:

Paranoid and Distrustful: Constantly wary of others and suspicious of hidden motives.
Reclusive and Withdrawn: Prefers solitude and isolation, finding solace within the confines of his home.
Protective of His Memories: Clings desperately to fragments of his past, despite the fading clarity of his recollections.

Backstory: Ashe Blackwood, a once vibrant and loving family man, now lives as a paranoid hermit, plagued by the cruel absence of his memories. He lost his entire family to the monstrous entity, Liv, yet his mind has shielded him from the trauma, leaving him tormented by a haunting void he cannot comprehend. In the depths of his secluded home, he wrestles with his fading sanity, surrounded by the remnants of a past he cannot remember.

Playstyle: Ashe is a character with a fragile mental state, making him susceptible to madness-inducing effects. His proficiency in Perception, Investigation, Survival, and Animal Handling reflects his keen awareness of his surroundings and his ability to survive in the wilderness. In combat, he relies on his staff and his loyal attack dogs to protect him from perceived threats. His cautious nature and distrust of others may lead to interesting interactions with other party members, as he constantly navigates the balance between protecting his fragmented memories and embracing the companionship they may bring.
