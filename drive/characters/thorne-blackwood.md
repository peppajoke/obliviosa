Character Name: Thorne Blackwood

*Thorne has no character sheet, because he dies as soon as the campaign begins.*

Race: Human
Class: Ranger
Background: Lumenshire Native

Physical Description: Thorne Blackwood is a rugged and wiry human with a weathered countenance, reflecting his life spent in the wilds. Standing at an average height, he possesses an athletic build honed by years of adventuring. Thorne's sun-kissed skin carries the faintest hint of freckles, testament to his countless hours spent under the island's warm sun. His hazel eyes shimmer with a mix of curiosity and determination, always scanning his surroundings with a keen awareness. His chestnut hair, usually tied back to keep it out of his face, falls in unruly waves, adding a touch of untamed charm to his appearance. Thorne carries himself with a quiet confidence, bearing the marks of a life shaped by adventure.

Background Story: Thorne Blackwood was born and raised in Lumenshire, his parents Ashe and Mary Blackwood providing a loving and nurturing home. Growing up, he had two brothers and one sister, a tight-knit family that shared many fond memories together. As a child, Thorne developed a deep connection with the natural world, finding solace and purpose in the untamed wilderness that surrounded Lumenshire.

The Disappearance: Tragically, Thorne's fate takes a dark turn at the beginning of the story, as he falls victim to the monstrous abomination that haunts Lumenshire. Devoured by the creature, his existence becomes a forgotten memory to his friends and companions, erasing all recollection of his presence. Unbeknownst to his friends, they embark on a journey to Lumenshire, accompanying Thorne, unaware of his fate, to check on his father, who has sent a disturbing letter.

Thorne's absence and forgotten existence cast a shadow over Lumenshire and the hearts of his friends, who embark on a perilous quest to uncover the truth behind his father's disturbing letter. As they journey through the enchanting island, Thorne's memory remains locked away, but his spirit lives on through the tales and adventures they shared, fueling their determination to confront the darkness that haunts Lumenshire and bring justice to their forgotten friend.

Thorne may have been romantically attached to Karleen.

# Meta

Thorne is the "fake" player character of the game. He is eaten by Liv at the beginning of the story, but the party does not remember him. The party will find a pool of his blood, his family blade, and [a letter.](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mQ_sNAvZ-a_fuzsoFcrMu29Mlm5b1_EnkCIZaQ6E3aM/edit)

Prior to the game, the player playing Thorne should be caught up on the basics, and will drop out of the game without explanation upon the first occurrence of the Hydra song.

Things the player should mention to the other players:

  - Family backstory is dark, and has a twist ending
  - His father has had a tough time
  - Thorne is a ranger. He's amazing at long range battle, but won't do great in close quarters.
  - Thorne has forgotten aspects of his past
