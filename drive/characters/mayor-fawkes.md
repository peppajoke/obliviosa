Character Name: Mayor Fawkes

Mayor Fawkes, a respected figure in Lumenshire, is hailed as a heroic savior who led a valiant group of adventurers against the menacing onslaught of The Forgotten. When the monstrous humanoid tentacle creatures emerged from the depths five years ago, terrorizing the town with their overwhelming power, it was Fawkes who rallied the frightened citizens and organized the courageous defense that drove the Forgotten back into the sea.

His inspiring leadership and unwavering courage during the three-day siege earned him the undying admiration of the townsfolk. They elected him as mayor, believing he was the embodiment of hope and protection against any future threats. Under his guidance, Lumenshire's military force, known as "The Luminous Vanguard," was formed, a disciplined and dedicated group charged with safeguarding the island and its people.

Fawkes' presence and leadership have since become synonymous with security and stability in Lumenshire. The townspeople regard him as a symbol of resilience and unity, with tales of his heroism passed down through generations. As mayor, he has consistently demonstrated a strong sense of duty, always striving to uphold justice and fairness for his constituents.

# Secret history

At the beginning of the story, Fawkes is contemplating suicide, unable to bear the horrific secrets of his past, and what has become of his daughter, convinced her and himself are beyond saving.

In the hallowed halls of justice, Fawkes stood before the court on trial for the murder of his wife. As the proceedings reached their peak, a chilling silence enveloped the courtroom, broken only by a haunting incantation that emanated from his young daughter.

With trembling hands, Liv cast the Oblivion spell, hoping to wipe away the memories of her father's alleged crimes from the minds of the people of Lumenshire. But the magic twisted and warped, engulfing her in a horrifying transformation. In an instant, she became a monstrous abomination with three heads, unleashing chaos upon the courthouse.

The once majestic hall became a battlefield, and Fawkes found himself torn between the love for his daughter and the duty to protect the innocent. Liv, in her monstrous form, began devouring countless citizens of Lumenshire, erasing their memories from those who knew them. The rampage was unforgiving, and the tragedy unfolded before Fawkes' tear-filled eyes.

When hope seemed to be fading, a glimmer of courage ignited within the hearts of Fawkes and a band of rallied civilians. Together, they took up arms against the monstrous menace and the menacing Forgotten that accompanied Liv. Yet, when it came time to confront his daughter, Fawkes found himself unable to raise a hand against her.

With heavy hearts, they fought off the Forgotten, driving what remained of them back to their dark lair, the Forgotten Reach. Fearing the threat they posed, they destroyed the bridge leading back to the mainland, hoping to isolate the menace and buy time to seek a permanent solution.

In the aftermath of the carnage, the people of Lumenshire began to forget, one by one, their memories fading like wisps of smoke in the wind. Liv, now a monstrous entity, scaled the islands every night, singing a haunting song of oblivion, erasing all recollection of her and her father's alleged crimes from the minds of the people.

As the tale of the Forgotten's mysterious return spread, Fawkes emerged as a hero, leading the people to victory against the malevolent creatures. A group of loyal followers soon formed the Luminous Vanguard, a devoted military faction pledged to Fawkes' leadership and the protection of Lumenshire.

However, amidst the apparent amnesia that shrouded Lumenshire, Fawkes remained the only one who remembered the truth—the terrible events that unfolded, the loss of innocent lives, and the tragic transformation of his beloved daughter.

In the shadows of the night, Liv, now in her monstrous form, sought sustenance from the memories of the people she devoured. Every few nights, Fawkes facilitated these sacrifices, an uneasy truce born from the desperate desire to protect his daughter and the people he had sworn to serve.

As Liv and the Forgotten returned periodically, Fawkes reluctantly provided a captive sacrifice, their memories consumed by the monstrous abomination. An uneasy peace settled between them, and they retreated to the depths beneath the ruins of the Forgotten Reach, where darkness and secrets lay in wait.

Now, Fawkes walks a path fraught with sorrow and determination, for his heart knows that only by finding a permanent solution can he hope to save his daughter and the people of Lumenshire from this malevolent cycle of oblivion and despair. But the price of this truth weighs heavily on his soul, as he continues to protect the secrets buried deep within the ruins of his daughter's monstrous form.

# Character sheet

Name: Fawkes
Race: Human
Class: Fighter
Background: Noble
Alignment: Lawful Evil
Level: 10

Ability Scores: STR 16 (+3), DEX 14 (+2), CON 16 (+3), INT 12 (+1), WIS 14 (+2), CHA 15 (+2)
Proficiency Bonus: +4
Armor Class: 18 (Chain Mail + Shield)
Hit Points: 85
Hit Dice: 10d10
Speed: 30 ft.
Saving Throws: Strength +7, Constitution +7
Skills: Athletics +7, Persuasion +6, History +4, Insight +4

Features and Traits:

Fighting Style (Protection): When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.
Second Wind: As a bonus action, Fawkes can regain 1d10 + 10 hit points.
Action Surge: Once per short rest, Fawkes can take an additional action on his turn.
Improved Critical: Fawkes' weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.

Equipment: Longsword (+1): 1d8 + 3 slashing damage; Shield; Chain Mail; Explorers Pack; Symbol of Office (Mayor of Lumenshire); Various trinkets and keepsakes from past adventures

Background: Born into a noble family with a long history of valor and service, Fawkes was raised to uphold a strong sense of justice and responsibility. He trained from a young age in the ways of combat and leadership, honing his skills as a fighter and developing a keen sense of strategy. As the heir to the family's legacy, Fawkes felt the weight of his name, determined to make his ancestors proud.

Personality Traits:

Noble and Honorable: Fawkes believes in the importance of integrity and doing what is right, even in the face of adversity.
Protective: He has a deep sense of responsibility for the people of Lumenshire and will do whatever it takes to keep them safe.
Resilient: Fawkes carries the burden of his past actions and strives to learn from his mistakes and grow stronger.

Backstory: Fawkes' life took an unexpected turn when he fell in love with a woman named Elara, a historian and witch with an insatiable curiosity for the forbidden and arcane. Despite their love for each other, their marriage was fraught with challenges, as Elara's fascination with The Forgotten and her struggles with mental instability put a strain on their relationship.

One tragic day, in a fit of rage and desperation to protect their daughter, Liv, from Elara's cruelty, Fawkes accidentally took his wife's life. The event led to a dark secret that he has since buried deep within himself. Determined to protect Liv and clear his name, Fawkes took the blame and was put on trial for the murder of his wife.

Secret Motivation: Unbeknownst to the people of Lumenshire, Fawkes is haunted by the guilt of what transpired with his wife and the tragic transformation of his daughter, Liv, into the monstrous hydra. His public persona is a facade, a mask of strength and heroism to keep the town united and safe. He secretly yearns to find a way to undo the spell and free Liv from her monstrous form, even if it means seeking forbidden knowledge or making sacrifices of his own.
