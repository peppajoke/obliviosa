# The Forgotten

SUPER WEAK TO LIGHTNING. Advantage on hit and damage.

Physical Description:

The Forgotten are nightmarish aberrations, towering at an intimidating ten feet in height. Their twisted and grotesque forms are covered in slimy, mottled gray skin, pulsating with eerie, bioluminescent patterns that glow in the darkness. Numerous long and sinewy tentacles sprout from their elongated limbs, their writhing movements creating an unsettling sight. Their eyes, a piercing and malevolent shade of crimson, seem to emanate an unnatural aura that chills the hearts of those who behold them.

Historic Description:

Legends speak of an ancient and malevolent force that had long been forgotten by the world, banished by ancient sorcerers for their insatiable desire for destruction and chaos. For ages, they remained imprisoned in the abyss, tales of their existence fading into dark folklore and terrifying bedtime stories.

During Liv's dark ritual, the Forgotten were given renewed passage into our realm, once again roaming the shores of Lumenshire for fresh victims of their dark torment.

The Forgotten regard Liv as a sort of goddess, but she exerts no explicit control over them. The Forgotten remain an enigmatic enemy of the people of Lumenshire, including Fawkes.

**The big twist is that the Forgotten were actually people that have been twisted through memory magic so badly that they have become corrupted. The cultists turn into these eventually.**

# Character sheet

Name: The Forgotten
Race: Aberration
Alignment: Chaotic Evil
Size: Large
Hit Points: 150 (15d10 + 75)
Speed: 30 ft.
Armor Class: 16 (Natural Armor)

Strength: 20 (+5)
Dexterity: 14 (+2)
Constitution: 20 (+5)
Intelligence: 12 (+1)
Wisdom: 10 (+0)
Charisma: 6 (-2)

Saving Throws: Strength +9, Constitution +9
Skills: Perception +4, Stealth +5
Senses: Darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14
Languages: Understands Common but can't speak
Damage Immunities: Psychic
Condition Immunities: Charmed, Frightened

Special Abilities:

Tentacle Attack: Melee Weapon Attack, +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 3d8 + 5 bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 17). The Forgotten can use its tentacles to grapple up to two targets simultaneously.

Memory Drain: When a target is grappled by The Forgotten, it can use its action to drain the memories of the grappled creature. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or take 3d6 psychic damage and have its memories of the past 24 hours erased. The Forgotten gains temporary hit points equal to the damage dealt. A creature that succeeds on the saving throw takes half damage and retains its memories.

Incorporeal Movement: The Forgotten can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

Fearful Presence: Creatures that start their turn within 30 feet of The Forgotten must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. The frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

Actions:

Multiattack: The Forgotten makes two tentacle attacks.

Swallow: The Forgotten attempts to swallow a grappled creature of Medium size or smaller. The grappled target must succeed on a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw or be swallowed. A swallowed creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside The Forgotten. The swallowed creature takes 6d6 acid damage at the start of each of The Forgotten's turns. If The Forgotten takes 30 damage or more on a single turn from a creature inside it, it must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate the swallowed creature, which falls prone in a space within 10 feet of The Forgotten.

Legendary Actions:

The Forgotten can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Forgotten regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

Memory Absorption: The Forgotten targets one creature it can see within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or lose one randomly determined prepared spell or one use of a spell slot. The Forgotten gains temporary hit points equal to the spell's level.

Nightmarish Presence: The Forgotten lets out an otherworldly screech. Each creature within 30 feet of it that can hear it must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened for 1 minute. The frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

Move: The Forgotten moves up to half its speed.
