# Fawkes intro

As the party approached the door to Fawkes' office, they couldn't help but notice the grandeur of the building that housed the Mayor of Lumenshire. The town hall stood tall and imposing, its stone walls adorned with intricate carvings depicting the history of the island city. As they entered, the heavy wooden door creaked behind them, and they found themselves in a spacious, lavishly decorated antechamber.

Tapestries of vibrant colors adorned the walls, depicting epic battles fought and won, and intricate chandeliers dangled from the ornate ceiling, casting a warm and inviting glow. The floor beneath their feet was polished to a pristine shine, reflecting the flickering flames of sconces that lined the walls.

At the far end of the antechamber, an exquisite set of double doors stood tall and imposing, the symbol of Lumenshire etched into their surface. Two guards, their armor gleaming in the soft light, stood on either side, their vigilant eyes surveying the room. As the party approached, the guards nodded in acknowledgment, one of them reaching out to knock on the ornate doors.

The sound of the knock echoed through the chamber, and a deep, authoritative voice called from within, "Enter."

The doors opened, and the party stepped into the heart of Fawkes' domain. His office was equally grand, with floor-to-ceiling windows offering a breathtaking view of the city and the distant sea. Sunlight poured through the glass, illuminating the room with a radiant glow. A large wooden desk, covered with meticulously arranged documents and parchments, sat at the center of the chamber.

Behind the desk sat Mayor Fawkes himself, a figure of regal presence, with greying hair framing his weathered face. His eyes, a mesmerizing shade of deep blue, sparkled with intelligence and determination. His golden and silver armor glistens in the sunlight, with his dark blue cloak a pleasing visual compliment. As he finishes writing important notes on his parchment, he abruptly signals his guards to leave the room, and glances up at the party…

"I was saddened to hear of your unfortunate arrival, adventurers. While fate seems to have scorned you, it seems to have smiled upon me…I believe we can help each other. The threat of the Forgotten still looms over the Isles of Lumenshire, but I have a plan—a plan that, I dare say, will save us from their menacing grasp once and for all," Fawkes continued, his eyes shining with hope. "With the help of brave adventurers like you, I believe we can save the souls of my beloved home from a painful, terrible fate. My people are happy because they know not how close they are to oblivion. They go about their happy lives, and we lie to them. We hide the terrifying truths that would destroy their well being, the way a father protects his daughter from the horrors of the world. The Forgotten lurk among us, I can sense it. I drove the bastards back years ago, and I can feel their presence once again closing in. My plan is bold and most will not understand, but after years of contemplation, I know that this is the only way to spare my people from the pain and suffering that lies ahead. Adventurers, I have developed a serum that when drank, will shed the Forgotten's disguises. I know they live among us. My plan is to serve the people of Lumenshire this elixir that will reveal the true nature of these secret devils. The true citizens of Lumenshire will be unaffected by this potion, where those hiding among us will be revealed. Help me by retrieving these potions and I will mix them into the celebratory ale at the Remembrance Festival this week, and I will grant you passage home. What say you?"

**Let players respond.**

"This errand must be done in complete secrecy, we do not know who among us can be trusted. Anyone who witnesses your actions must be incapacitated until the end of the festival. I will not tolerate my plan being discovered by the Forgotten."

**Fawkes hands each player a marble.**

"These marbles will allow the one time imprisonment of an individual who has been incapacitated. Should you be discovered, use non lethal force to subdue the witness, imprison them in your marble, and bring them back here, along with the shipment of potions."

## Party agrees

"Thank you. Together we can save my homeland, and get you back home."

## Party declines

"I'm afraid this isn't a request. You can either do this for me, or you're never leaving Lumenshire. I cannot risk the integration of this plan, you understand." **Fawkes signals for his guards to enter the room. The party is surrounded by heavily armed Luminous Vanguard Knights.**

"I'll ask you once more, what say you?"

**If they still decline, roll initiative. They will be captured if they lose.**
