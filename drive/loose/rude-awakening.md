# Narration

As your consciousness slowly returns, you awaken prone on a sandy beach, the remains of your ship strewn across the rocks and sand. Cid is nowhere to be found.

Your eyes settle on a pool of crimson liquid nearby, its surface reflecting the dappled sunlight filtering through the canopy. The blood looks thick, almost viscous, and you notice a mysterious object submerged within it, partially hidden from view. The sight raises more questions than answers..

In the distance, the silhouette of a modest farmstead catches your eye, its smoke-filled chimney dancing in the breeze. The sight offers a glimmer of civilization amid the untamed wilderness.

On the other side, a well-worn path beckons, leading toward what appears to be a city or town bustling with life.

# Farmhouse

The farmhouse stands tall and proud, its white-washed walls gleaming in the sun. From a distance, it looks like a picture-perfect slice of rural Neverwinter, with its red roof and green shutters adding a pop of color to the serene landscape. There is a signpost near the door that reads "Blackwood".

The yard is overgrown with weeds and wildflowers, their tangled stems reaching out like grasping fingers. The windows, although clean and free of cracks, seem to shimmer with a candlelight, seemingly from the inside of the house. It seems someone is inside.
