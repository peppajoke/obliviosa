# Setting up

This is a reality bending campaign. I may gaslight you because your character may not know things, or may be experiencing reality in a particular way. If I'm saying something that doesn't add up, that means it's probably part of the mystery you need to solve. This is a complex story, with many very powerful players who are stronger than you. You'll want to make allies, uncover the mystery, and decide how you want to resolve the story. You can do whatever you want!
