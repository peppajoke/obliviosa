# Willem's Forge

*As you step into the forge, the first thing that hits you is the intense heat emanating from the roaring furnace in the center of the room. The furnace is surrounded by anvils and workbenches, each littered with various tools and implements of the blacksmith's trade. The air is thick with the acrid smell of burning coal and the sharp tang of hot metal.*

*The forge is a dimly lit, cavernous space, with walls made of rough-hewn stone and a high, vaulted ceiling that echoes with the sounds of clanging metal and the rhythmic pounding of hammers on anvils. The flickering light of the furnace casts long shadows across the walls, giving the whole space a mysterious and otherworldly feel.*

*At the back of the forge, seated at a small desk surrounded by stacks of leather-bound journals and scrolls, is Willem, the master blacksmith. Willem is a grizzled old man, with a thick beard and calloused hands that have seen years of hard labor. Despite his gruff exterior, he has a kind heart and a wealth of knowledge about the art of blacksmithing.*

*As you wander through the forge, you notice a half-finished sword lying on one of the workbenches, its blade only partially shaped and the handle wrapped in a rough leather grip its quality seems to stand out to you as quite inferior compared to everything else on display. Nearby, a set of tongs lies abandoned on the floor, as if dropped in haste. On the wall above the anvil, you spot a faded portrait of a young man with a mischievous grin, his arm draped over Willem's shoulder.*

*Willem is a blacksmith in Lumenshire and is known for his exceptional work. Willem is in his mid-40s and has been a resident of the village for his entire life. He's a bit gruff and often complains about the younger generation not respecting the craft of blacksmithing. Willem has had to put much more manual effort into his work as of late, and his capacity has diminished. He does not know why, but the work seems much harder lately, and he is putting more hours into his smithing and cannot put his finger on why that is.*

If asked about the portrait, Willem becomes disoriented and frightened. He suggests that the players put it there, and asks them to leave. He is heard crying after the players leave the forge.
