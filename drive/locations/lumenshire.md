If the players are with Karleen, they can cheese past the guards. Otherwise, they will be questioned. If they act suspicious they will be detained by the Luminous Vanguard and brought to Seraphina.

## Geography and Layout

Lumenshire is a secluded island located far off the coast of the city of Neverwinter. The island is split into four main islands, three of which are connected by bridges.

Mysterious Island: The smallest island, located in the southeastern part, is no longer accessible due to the destroyed bridge. The building there, heavily damaged during the Forgotten's attack, holds a shroud of mystery, as various rumors circulate about its past purpose.

The Forgotten's Attack:

Lumenshire faced a tragic event five years ago when the monstrous humanoid tentacle creatures known as The Forgotten returned to wreak havoc. They launched a brutal attack over a three-day period, causing widespread destruction on the southwestern merchant district and abducting citizens. The town was saved through the efforts of a man named Fawkes, who organized a group of brave adventurers to fight the Forgotten and push them back into the sea.

Fawkes and His Military Force:

Fawkes, the heroic figure who led the successful defense against The Forgotten, was later elected as the mayor of Lumenshire. His military force, known as "The Luminous Vanguard," became the protectors of the island. The Vanguard is a disciplined and dedicated group, dressing in deep blue and silver armor adorned with luminescent sigils, enhancing their intimidating appearance.

Appearance and Demeanor:

The members of The Luminous Vanguard have an air of mystique about them, giving off an almost cult-like aura. They move with eerie precision and seldom show emotion, instilling a sense of unwavering loyalty to their cause and leader. Their eyes often appear to gleam with an otherworldly light, earning them the moniker of "Luminous." The combination of their imposing presence and glowing accents contributes to the whispered rumors among the townsfolk, painting them as an enigmatic force.

Despite this, the people of Lumenshire trust and revere The Luminous Vanguard, for they remember the heroism displayed during the Forgotten's assault. The Vanguard maintains constant patrols throughout the islands, instilling a sense of safety and security among the populace, even as the looming threat of another Forgotten attack hangs over the town.
