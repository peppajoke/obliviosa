# The Dragon's Inn

*As you enter the Dragon's Inn, the warm glow of the hearth and the lively chatter of patrons wash over you. The scent of roasting meat and freshly baked bread wafts through the air, making your stomach growl with hunger.*

*Beatrice, the middle-aged innkeeper, stands behind the bar with a welcoming smile, greeting customers and expertly pouring drinks. The walls are adorned with dragon tapestries and carvings, and the low ceiling is hung with lanterns that cast a dim light over the scene.*

*Despite the cozy atmosphere, you can't help but notice that the inn seems a bit larger than necessary for the number of patrons present. The wooden beams that support the ceiling are thick and sturdy, and there are many more tables and chairs than there are customers.*

*As you take a seat and order some food and drink, you hear a group of boisterous halflings playing a game of dice at a nearby table, their laughter ringing out through the tavern. The sound of a bard's lute fills the air, providing a cheerful accompaniment to the jovial conversations and clinking glasses around you. You settle in to enjoy the lively atmosphere and delicious fare of the Dragon's Inn.*

*As you approach the bar, Beatrice greets you with a warm smile and says, "Welcome to the Dragon's Inn, travelers! What can I get for you? Never seen you folk around, need a room? Only 10 gold for the night."*

## For sale (everything is 1 gold)

Dragon's Breath Ale - a dark, hoppy ale with a smoky finish
Pixie Punch - a fruity, sweet cocktail with a kick of vodka
Hydra's Fury - a potent cocktail made with several types of liquor, including a splash of dragonfire whiskey, known to make even the toughest adventurer forget their worries
Elvish Wine - a light, crisp white wine with a floral aroma
Dwarven Stout - a thick, rich beer with a hint of chocolate and coffee
Faerie Nectar - a sweet, sparkling wine made from the honey of enchanted flowers
Unicorn's Blood - a deep red wine with a fruity, almost floral flavor and a subtle hint of magic.

## Patrons (revise to have them all lead towards the cave, or restoration of memories)

**Jack**

A burly man with a thick beard and calloused hands, wearing a leather apron and a tool belt. He has clearly just come from the nearby smithy and is sipping on a dark ale while wiping his brow with a dirty cloth.

He's in a good mood and happy to chat with the party. If they ask about his work, he'll proudly show them some of the tools he's made.

**Marcus**

A finely dressed nobleman with a haughty demeanor, sitting at a corner table and sipping on a glass of red wine, while his servant cuts his food for him. He keeps glancing around the room with a look of disdain, as if he finds himself above the other patrons.

A local noble who's come to town to check on his estate. He's happy to chat with the party, but can be a bit condescending. If they ask about his estate, he'll likely talk at length about his many holdings. He hasn't been in town long, and his servant is DEAF! The party only discovers this if they ask or try to interact with him.

**Servantboy Carl**

The servant boy Carl is small and thin, with dirt smudged across his face and ragged clothing that hangs loosely off his frame. He has a mop of unkempt hair that falls across his forehead, and his eyes dart nervously around the room as he goes about his duties. Despite his disheveled appearance, there's a certain resilience in the way he carries himself, perhaps a result of his harsh upbringing. He is deaf. Carl has seen the hydra and remembers it, but only in the distance. He didn't see it eat anyone or anything nuts.

**Sarah**

A middle-aged woman, sitting alone at the bar and nursing a glass of whiskey. She appears antisocial and seems to be drinking quickly, her eyes fixed on some distant point. She looks distraught and lost in thought, and doesn't seem interested in talking to anyone. Has no memory of her life outside of her name. She's horrified that she's lost her mind and will likely be hesitant to speak to the party. If they persist, she may become agitated or even angry.

**Thomas**

An old man with a bushy white beard, his face lined with wrinkles and his eyes twinkling with mischief. He is sipping on a mug of mead and regaling a group of young adventurers with stories of his own past exploits, some of which may be true and some of which are undoubtedly embellished.

A grizzled old man with an eye patch, is a retired sailor who's settled down in the town. He's got plenty of stories to tell about his time at sea and is happy to share them with anyone who will listen. If the party seems interested, he might even offer to take them out on his boat for a short trip.
