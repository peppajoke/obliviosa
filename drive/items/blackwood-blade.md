Swift Blade of the Blackwood Family:

The Swift Blade of the Blackwood Family is a slender and elegant blade with a silvery sheen. Etched with glowing elven runes along the fuller, it bears the Blackwood family crest on its blackwood hilt. This legendary weapon enhances the wielder's agility and speed in combat, cutting through the air effortlessly. It carries the weight of generations and symbolizes the Blackwood family's dedication to protecting Lumenshire.

Weapon Type: Longsword
Damage: 1d8 slashing
Properties: Finesse, Versatile (1d10)
Bonus: +1 to attack and damage rolls
Special Ability: Once per short rest, the wielder can use a bonus action to gain advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Dexterity (Stealth) checks for one minute.
Rarity: Rare
