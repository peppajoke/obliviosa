# 01. The opening: a letter found in blood

Direction (Jack, Oct 2025): the campaign starts with the party finding the letter in blood. The person the letter is addressed to is the friend they have already lost and already forgotten.

This was always the design (Rude Awakening + Thorne Blackwood + A letter found in blood). What changes here: it is the **first thing that happens**, not the back half of a session. No Flaming Fist oath, no salvage contract, no Salt Wind toast on screen. Those can exist as backstory the players never get to replay, which is exactly the point.

## Before the session: who is the forgotten friend

Three ways to run it. Pick one. **Recommended: A if a confederate is available, otherwise B.**

**A. The planted player (original design).** A friend of the group plays Thorne for a short pre-wreck prologue (fifteen minutes: the Salt Wind toast, Cid's bottle, "tell the story of the time..."). When the song plays, the Thorne player gets up and leaves and does not come back. The other players will remember him; their characters will not. Jack keeps a straight face. Best possible payoff. Needs one confederate who can keep a secret for a month.

**B. Cold open, no confederate.** The session begins with the song in the dark. The players never meet Thorne. They find the letter, the blade, the blood. The forgotten friend is a hole the players themselves have to notice. Easier to run; the horror lands later (Marcus/Karleen's "DON'T FORGET" notes describe the party perfectly and describe a fifth person).

**C. Replace Thorne** (Jack: "might replace him"). Options that keep the letter beat intact:
- Make the forgotten friend **Cid**. The letter is to Cid from his father instead; Cid is "nowhere to be found" after the wreck anyway. Cost: Cid is needed later for the airship escape and the "reconnect with Cid in town" beat.
- Make the forgotten friend **a PC's own backstory character**: in session zero each player names one NPC who matters to their character (a mentor, a sibling, a rival). One of those is the letter's addressee. Most personal, most work.
- Keep Thorne but **retitle him**: Ashe's son is whoever the players decide brought them here. Ask at the table, once, before the song: "Whose idea was this trip?" Take the answer and make that person the addressee. Then play the song.

Whatever the choice, three facts stay fixed: the friend is Ashe Blackwood's son, the friend carried the family blade, the friend was eaten by Liv during the wreck and is why the wreck happened.

## Cold open (run this)

**0. Lights down.** Play the Hydra song. Say nothing until it ends. Everyone rolls 2d6 and takes that damage. If running option A, the Thorne player leaves during the song.

**1. The beach.** Read:

> As your consciousness slowly returns, you awaken prone on a sandy beach, the remains of your ship strewn across the rocks and sand. Cid is nowhere to be found.
>
> Your eyes settle on a pool of crimson nearby, its surface reflecting the dappled sunlight through the canopy. The blood is thick, almost viscous, and something is submerged in it, partially hidden from view.
>
> In the distance, the silhouette of a modest farmstead, its chimney smoking. On the other side, a well-worn path toward what looks like a town.

**2. The pool.** In it: the **Swift Blade of the Blackwood Family** (longsword, finesse, +1, family crest on a blackwood hilt) and a **letter**, soaked but legible. Hand the players the letter as a physical prop. It begins "My Beloved son Thorne" and is signed "Your loving father, Ashe Blackwood". Full text in `../drive/found-documents/a-letter-found-in-blood.md`.

Optional, and worth it: scratched into the rock beside the pool, in blood, a single line that trails off: **"DON'T FORGET M"**. The last letters are a smear where the hand was pulled away.

**3. What checks give them** (let them earn it, do not volunteer):

| Check | DC | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Medicine | 12 | Human blood, less than an hour old, far too much for anyone to have walked away from. No body. Drag marks lead into the surf. |
| Investigation (beach) | 13 | One more set of footprints than there are party members. They come from the wreck to the pool and stop. |
| Investigation (wreck) | 14 | One more bedroll, one more mess kit, one more hammock than the party needs. The ship's manifest lists a passenger count one higher than the party. The name on that line is water-ruined. |
| Arcana (blade) | 15 | The runes are still warm. It was attuned to someone until very recently. |
| Insight (self) | 10 | You are grieving and you do not know for whom. The feeling fades as you try to look at it. |
| History / Society | 15 | You have heard of Lumenshire and cannot remember why. On a 20+: you know you should remember more about this island, and do not. |

**4. Two roads.** The farmhouse (signpost: "Blackwood") or the town. Both are written: farmhouse in `../drive/loose/rude-awakening.md` (Ashe, two dogs, flintlock, Persuasion DC 25, "I TOLD THAT DIRTY OLD MAN FAWKES I AIN'T LEAVIN"), town in `../drive/notes/into-lumenshire.md`.

If they go to the farmhouse first: Ashe reads the letter and does not recognise his own handwriting. Then he does. Then he asks which of them is Thorne. That is the first time anyone says the name aloud, and nobody at the table should be able to answer.

If they go to town first: they will be summoned to Fawkes or found by Karleen before they get back to the farm, and Ashe becomes the mid-act gut punch instead of the opening one. Either order works.

## Mechanic to carry forward: the memory cards

The letter beat only works once. To keep the forgetting playable all campaign:

- At session zero each player writes 6 to 8 index cards: one fact per card about their character's relationships, history, and the party. "My sister Mara taught me the bow." "We met Cid in Luskan." "I owe Thorne my life."
- Each time the song plays, the DM collects one card per player, face down, chosen by the DM. The player does not get to re-read it. From then on, that fact is not true for their character and the DM plays the world accordingly (the NPC does not exist, the place has a different name).
- Marcus/Karleen's "DON'T FORGET" pages are the in-world version of the same mechanic and should be shown to the players as such.
- Killing Liv returns every card. Saving her does not.

## What the opening must set up (checklist)

- [ ] The name Thorne is spoken once, by Ashe or by the letter, and lands on nobody.
- [ ] The party holds the Blackwood blade. Someone will want to give it back to Ashe. Let that be a scene.
- [ ] Cid is missing. He is alive, in town, and does not remember the wreck clearly either.
- [ ] The party has no ship and no money for one. The island has them.
- [ ] Fawkes's re-election posters are the first thing they see in town. "Ten more years."
