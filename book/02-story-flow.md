## Act 2: Into Lumenshire

- Arrival. If they walk in without Karleen the Vanguard questions them; acting suspicious gets them hauled to Seraphina.
- Town texture: election posters everywhere, a little boy who will not shut up about re-election, memorial displays in the residential square, graffiti reading "Fuck the Forgotten", the oversized graveyard, the bard's unexplained sad songs, strange symbols appearing on walls.
- The six shops. Each shopkeeper carries one hole (see canon). Gary's shop is where Seraphina buys her no-dream gummies and where the anti-song tools are sold. The Mystic's Emporium is ransacked and still needs a clue planted in it. **[TODO]**
- Cid is in town. He survived. He wants a ship (5k) or the weekly ferry, and he is already talking about building something that flies.
- **Karleen** watches them (Perception DC 15), then approaches with the DON'T FORGET notes that describe the party and a fifth person named Thorne. She has rented a room at the Dragon's Inn as a base. **[Q4: this speech is still written as Marcus's; needs rewriting in Karleen's voice]**
- Tavern patrons seed the cave / the Forgotten Reach: Thomas the sailor, Sarah who has forgotten her life, Carl the deaf servant boy who has seen the hydra.
- First night: the song plays. Fade to black. They wake at the inn with wounds they cannot explain. Off-screen, Liv fed and the cultists came through.
- Morning: a shop that was not there yesterday (The Enchanted Garden, Perception DC 20). Townsfolk waking in the street.
- Act 2 finale per the redux: **a Forgotten wanders into town** in daylight. The party fights it in front of everyone. It carries a clue pointing at Fawkes.

## Act 3: Pick a side

The redux wants the players to commit to a track early. Three doors, each with its own reveal:

| Track | Shape | Reveals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Karleen** | Classic dungeon. Treehouse reveal (her evidence room: drawings of a family she does not remember), then break into Fawkes's library / the secret lair under town hall. | Fawkes killed his wife. Fawkes had a daughter. He owns the Oblivion tome. Legal records of the trial. His suicide note. Documents about Karleen's family he meant to give her someday. |
| **Fawkes** | Intrigue and deception. Seraphina summons them (refuse and they are beaten unconscious and brought anyway). Fawkes's office speech, the marbles that imprison one witness each, the Remembrance Festival serum errand. Declining means never leaving the island. | Erasure of evidence. Someone saw something; break into their house, steal documents. Who the "potion pickup" rivals are. |
| **The cult / the cave** | Sneaking and fighting. Ashe's clues or cultist trails lead to the cult cave on the Forgotten Reach. | How to beat the monster. The cultists are becoming Forgotten. Hydra spawn in the nest. |

Seraphina can "deliver justice" as an Act 3 opener if the players have made themselves a problem.
