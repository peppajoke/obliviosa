# 00. Canon: the truth of Lumenshire

This campaign runs as a dream inside another campaign with an existing party; see `06-dream-frame.md` for the entry point, persistence and past/future rules. The island story below is the same regardless of frame.

Everything below is DM-only truth, merged from every source doc. Where sources disagree, the choice made here is marked **[decision]** and the alternative is listed in `03-open-questions.md`.

## The island

Lumenshire sits far off the coast of Neverwinter **[decision: Neverwinter, not Baldur's Gate; see Q7]**. Four islands:

- **Residential District** (NE, largest). Town hall, church spires, central square with the sea-themed fountain, memorial displays, the graveyard that is far too big for the town.
- **Merchant District** (SW). Grand marble trading hall, warehouses, and the six shops: Apothe-gary (Gary), Willem's Forge, Gwendolyn's Garments, Bartholomew's Bakery, The Dragon's Inn (Beatrice), The Mystic's Emporium (ransacked, abandoned). Scarred buildings from "the attack".
- **Lumen Pier** (NW). The only port. Ships are rare. A return passage costs 1000g a head or a 5k ship; the ferry comes once a week. There is a hidden graveyard of destroyed boats somewhere on the coast; Karleen knows where.
- **Forgotten Reach** (SE, smallest). Bridge destroyed five years ago. The ruined courthouse is here. Liv's lair is beneath it. Nobody goes.

The Blackwood farmstead is outside town, on the beach side of the road.

## What actually happened (five years ago)

1. Fawkes married **Elara**, a historian-witch obsessed with the Forgotten and descended from the ancient wizard who first opened the void portal that let them in. She was cruel to their daughter **Liv**.
2. Fawkes came home to find Elara drowning Liv in the lake. He killed Elara in the struggle.
3. Fawkes was tried for murder in the courthouse on the SE island. Nine-year-old Liv, trying to save him, cast **Oblivion** (7th-level necromancy, from Elara's books) on the courtroom to make everyone forget his crime.
4. The spell failed. Liv became a thirty-foot blue hydra. She devoured the courtroom and much of the town. Everyone she eats is erased from the memory of everyone who knew them. The botched spell also reopened the way for **the Forgotten**.
5. Fawkes and a militia drove the Forgotten back to the SE island and blew the bridge. Fawkes could not strike his daughter.
6. The town forgot. Fawkes became the hero of "the Forgotten's attack" and was elected mayor. He founded the **Luminous Vanguard** under **Seraphina Dawnbringer**.
7. For five years Fawkes has kept an uneasy truce: every few nights he delivers a captive to Liv so she feeds without rampaging. He is the only person who remembers everything. At campaign start he is suicidal and planning the Remembrance Festival "serum" (see Fawkes Missions), which is really a mass dose meant to flush out... whatever he tells the party it is. **[Q3: what the serum actually does is undefined in source]**

## The song and the forgetting (table rules)

- Liv **sings**. Anyone who hears the song is affected. Table convention: play the Hydra song track, every player rolls 2d6 and takes it as damage, the DM edits reality while it plays.
- Being eaten erases the victim from memory. Evidence remains: belongings, portraits, receipts, extra chairs, an oversized graveyard, sad songs the bard cannot explain.
- The DM may lie to players about what their characters know. Session-zero disclaimer (from the redux script): "If I'm saying something that doesn't add up, that's probably part of the mystery."
- Resisting the song, as currently written: Potion of Encapsulation (Gary), Balm of the Banshee (Gary), a tuning fork or other loud noise, a Potion of Deafness Gary can be talked into making exactly once (may be permanent), playing an instrument (Wis DC 10 to keep memory), the Staff of Silence, the Orb of Screaming, getting blackout drunk on Hydra's Fury, sneaking up on her while she is not singing. Ashe's "potion of clarity" gives advantage on Obliviosa checks.
- Killing Liv restores every lost memory on the island. Saving her through a willing keeper also restores them, at the cost of the keeper being forgotten (see `05-sacrifice-and-hooks.md`). Saving her any other way does not.
- **Blurred faces.** Any character who will later be eaten by Liv is shown to the players with a blurred face from their first appearance, in every handout and token. Never explained. See `06-dream-frame.md`.
- **Persistence.** Characters and inventory are never forked between the dream and the waking campaign. Continuity conflicts are Liv's illusions.

## Factions and motivations

- **Fawkes + Seraphina + Luminous Vanguard.** Keep the secret, keep Liv alive, keep Karleen out of it. Seraphina can be peeled away from Fawkes, but it is very hard.
- **Karleen and the rebels.** Uncover the truth at any cost. Her treehouse is the evidence room. She will push the party to raid Fawkes's library / the secret lair under town hall.
- **Liv and the Forgotten.** The Forgotten regard her as a goddess but she does not command them. Twist: the Forgotten are people, cultists and victims, corrupted by memory magic. Weak to lightning.
- **Elara** (redux only). Wants to reunite "her family" into one being, devour Lumenshire as fuel and ascend. Liv is half the plan. **[Q2: Elara is dead in every 2023 doc; the redux never says how she is back]**

## Endings (from the redux script, with Basic Script epilogues)

1. Fawkes's sacrifice.
2. A player's sacrifice.

4. Kill Elara, save Liv: no memories restored, Fawkes flees with his daughter.
5. Taken prisoner by the Forgotten.
6. Elara wins, everyone becomes Forgotten.
7. Party wipe: the party is forgotten by their own loved ones.
8. Escape by airship with Cid, unsolved, to be continued.
