# 04. Session 1 intro (sample, for feedback)

Premise fixed 2026-10-11: Kael Orendis grew up in Lumenshire. The letter was sent to Sal, as Kael, by Kael's father Ashe, who does not know his son is dead and his body taken, and has not seen him since. Sal did not come. The party does not remember the trip. Outside the dream, Sal will tell the party whatever they ask about Kael and the letter. Thorne is not in this run.

## Before the dream (Icespire DM, their scene)

Any knockout that takes the whole party at once. The one request: in the scene before it, Sal is separated from the group or in visible danger, and nothing is resolved. The last thing the players see of Sal is uncertainty.

## Cold open

Lights down. Play the song. Nobody speaks until it ends. Everyone rolls 2d6 and takes it as damage.

Read:

> Sand under your cheek. Salt in your mouth. You are on a beach you do not know, and the thing behind you on the rocks was a boat. Nobody is at the tiller. Nobody is anywhere.
>
> A few steps away the sand is dark and wet, and it is not water. Something is lying in it.
>
> Up the hill: a farmhouse, smoke from the chimney, a signpost. Down the shore: a road, and past it the roofs of a town.

They do not remember boarding a boat. They do not remember why they left Phandalin. Do not help.

## The signals on the beach

Lying in the blood: a folded letter, soaked, legible. Nothing else. No weapon, no pack. Hand it over as a prop. It begins **"My beloved son Kael"**, describes a wailing song and a house full of a family the writer cannot remember, and begs him to come home. Signed Ashe Orendis.

- **Headcount.** Let them do it themselves. They will come up one short and the only name that is missing is Sal's. No character can say a sixth person was ever on the boat. The players will. Let the silence sit.
- **The blood** (Medicine 12): human, under an hour old, far more than anyone walks away from. Drag marks into the surf. **The whole site is a perception void.** Passive Perception under 10: a pool of blood. 10 to 14: there was gear here, you cannot say what. 15+: someone was lying here and you cannot picture them. 20+: there is a person-shaped gap in your memory of the last hour.
- **The hull** (Investigation 14): a small coastal boat, not theirs. One hammock more than the party needs. A manifest with a passenger count one higher than them, the last name water-ruined.
- **The sending stone.** If anyone has one and tries it: the song, faintly, and nothing else. **[Jack: or does Sal answer?]**
- **History 15:** you have heard of Lumenshire and cannot remember why. 20+: you know you should remember more.

Nobody says "Kael" aloud yet. The letter does it for you.

## Three doors from the beach

**1. The farmhouse (Ashe Orendis).** Dogs, flintlock, DC 25 Persuasion or a fight. He screams that Fawkes sent them. Show him the letter and he goes quiet. He wrote it, he does not remember writing it, and then he does. He asks which of them is Kael. Hold on that. Nobody at the table can answer, and every player knows whose face Kael's is. Ashe has not seen his son in years. He remembers Kael leaving; he does not remember the family that left before him. The house is full of them. If the party is kind, he gives them Kael's room for the night and the first real clue: a child's drawing of a blue-haired girl, signed with a name that has been scratched out.

**2. The town.** Walk in and the Vanguard questions them. Fawkes posters everywhere. Karleen finds them in the square within the hour with the DON'T FORGET pages: five descriptions that fit the party exactly, and a sixth that has been crossed out so hard the parchment tore. She says she was supposed to meet them, and a man named Kael. She asks which of them he is. Second time the name lands on nobody.

**3. The water.** If they try to leave: the hull will not float. Cid is in town and wants 5,000 gold or a week for the ferry. The sea is the door that is not a door.

## Handling the choices

- **Farmhouse first.** Best order. Ashe is the gut punch, then town is the mystery. Karleen's pages hit harder after Ashe has already said the name.
- **Town first.** Fine. Ashe becomes the mid-session blow. The Vanguard summons them to Fawkes by evening either way.
- **Split the party.** Let them. Run the farmhouse and the square in parallel; the song that ends the session reunites them at the inn with no memory of walking there.
- **They attack Ashe.** He is level 4 and old. If he dies, the farmhouse is a perception void from then on and the letter is the only thing left of him. They did that. Do not soften it.
- **They ignore the letter.** Karleen has a copy of the same handwriting. Ashe has a dozen more letters never sent. The name finds them.
- **They decide it was Sal and grieve.** Correct. Encourage it. Do not confirm or deny. When they wake in Phandalin and Sal is standing there, that is a scene for the other table, and Sal will tell them about Kael if they ask.

## End of session 1

Whatever they are doing when it is time to stop: the song plays. Fade. They wake in the Dragon's Inn with cuts they do not remember and one thing in a pocket that was not there before (the cult plant). Collect one memory card each. Next session opens there.

## Open for Jack

- Does the sending stone reach Sal, and if so what does he hear?
- Does Ashe know his son's eyes do not match? (Sal's do not; Kael's did not.) If the party describes Sal to him, that is the moment he knows something is wrong.
