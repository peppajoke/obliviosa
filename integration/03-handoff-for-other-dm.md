# For the Icespire DM: the Lumenshire dream

One page. Everything the Icespire game needs to know, and nothing it does not.

**What it is.** Jack is running a side campaign, Lumenshire, as a dream the party has intermittently. Same characters, same sheets, same inventory. It runs for a session here and there when it suits both tables. Jack DMs those sessions.

**What you do.** At a moment of your choosing, knock the whole party out. All of them, at once, no save that matters. Cryovain's breath, an avalanche, a collapse in the excavation, poisoned ale at Gnomengarde, anything. Tell Jack the scene so the wake-up can echo it. They will wake on a beach somewhere else. Your campaign pauses at the knockout.

**Sal.** Jack cannot play Sal while DMing, so Sal is not in the dream. Before the knockout, imply indirectly that Sal is in danger or separated from the group, so that when they wake one short they assume the worst. Do not confirm anything. Sal is alive; the dream's reason for his absence is a plot point.

**Persistence.** Nothing is forked. If a player burns a potion in Phandalin between dream sessions, it is gone from the dream too. If they level up with you, they have it on the island. If this creates a contradiction, it is intended; the dream campaign is about memory loss and the contradictions are part of its horror. Please do not patch them.

**Loot and XP from the dream.** Whatever they earn on the island, they keep. Jack will send you a list after each dream session so your sheets stay true.

**How it ends, and what it means for you.** The dream resolves one of two ways, decided by whether any PC died in it.

- No PC died and nothing contradicted your game: the dream was the past. They wake from your knockout remembering it all, including where on the island they stashed their rewards and a weapon that can kill Cryovain. In that version the trip happened after Cryovain had already hit Phandalin and Sal stayed behind to hold the town together. Jack will give you the weapon's write-up and the stash list so the Cryovain finale can use them.
- Any PC died, or the dream broke continuity with your game: the dream was the future. They wake from your knockout with nothing, and the dreams stop for good. Your campaign runs to its finale. Afterwards, a letter arrives in Phandalin addressed to Kael Orendis (Sal will know what that means). That letter is how the party ends up on the island for real, and Jack takes it from there.
- Everyone died: it was only a dream. Nothing carries.

**Forgettable people.** Some nameless NPCs on the island will be described as oddly hard to notice or remember. That is deliberate. Players may ask you about it. Shrug.

**What Jack needs from you.** The knockout scene, the party's level at that moment, a yes to loot and XP carrying over, and, for the past version, whether you are comfortable with Cryovain having already attacked Phandalin before the trip. That is all.
