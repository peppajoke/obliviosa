# For the Icespire DM: the Lumenshire dream

One page. Everything the Icespire game needs to know, and nothing it does not.

**What it is.** Jack is running a side campaign, Lumenshire, as a dream the party has intermittently. Same characters, same sheets, same inventory. It runs for a session here and there when it suits both tables. Jack DMs those sessions.

**What you do.** At a moment of your choosing, knock the whole party out. All of them, at once, no save that matters. Cryovain's breath, an avalanche, a collapse in the excavation, poisoned ale at Gnomengarde, anything. Tell Jack the scene so the wake-up can echo it. They will wake on a beach somewhere else. Your campaign pauses at the knockout.

**Sal.** Jack cannot play Sal while DMing. Sal will not be present in the dream. Do not explain this to the players; the dream has its own reason and it is a plot point.

**Persistence.** Nothing is forked. If a player burns a potion in Phandalin between dream sessions, it is gone from the dream too. If they level up with you, they have it on the island. If this creates a contradiction, it is intended; the dream campaign is about memory loss and the contradictions are part of its horror. Please do not patch them.

**Loot and XP from the dream.** Whatever they earn on the island, they keep. Jack will send you a list after each dream session so your sheets stay true.

**How it ends, and what it means for you.** The dream resolves one of two ways, decided by whether any PC died in it.

- No PC died: the dream was the past. They wake up from your knockout remembering it all, including where on the island they stashed their rewards. Those stashes are real. At some point they will want to go find them; Lumenshire is an island off the Neverwinter coast and Jack can give you what you need.
- Any PC died: the dream was the future. They wake from your knockout with nothing. Your campaign is now heading toward that island, on whatever timeline you like.

**Blurred faces.** Jack will show some NPC art with blurred faces. That is deliberate. If a PC dies in the dream, their portrait will be blurred in later recaps too. Players will ask you about it. Shrug.

**What Jack needs from you.** The knockout scene, the party's level at that moment, and a yes to the loot and XP carrying over. That is all.
