# 04. Session 1 intro (rewritten 2026-10-13 to match `09-kael-returns.md`)

Runs straight after Nick's cut to black ("Kael?"). Built on the book's cold open (`../../book/01-opening-the-letter.md`) and the quest boards (`10-quest-board.md`).

What the DM knows and the players do not: Thorne brought Sal and the party to Lumenshire. On landing Liv ate Thorne, and the party forgot him and the reason for the trip. Then she saw her father and tore Sal out of Kael. Kael is at the temple of Ilmater, unconscious. Sal's soul is in Salanar's corpse on the Reach.

## Cold open

Lights down. Play the song. Nobody speaks until it ends. Everyone rolls 2d6 and takes it as damage.

Read:

> Sand under your cheek. Salt in your mouth. You are on a beach you do not know, and the thing behind you on the rocks was a boat. Nobody is at the tiller. Nobody is anywhere.
>
> A few steps away the sand is dark and wet, and it is not water. Something is lying in it.
>
> Up the hill: a farmhouse, smoke from the chimney, a signpost. Down the shore: a road, and past it the roofs of a town.

The last thing the players saw was a stranger saying "Kael?" to Sal in Phandalin. Their characters do not remember boarding a boat, or why they left. Do not help.

## The pool

Lying in the blood: the **Swift Blade of the Blackwood Family** and a **letter**, soaked but legible. Hand the letter over as a prop. It is the book's letter, unchanged: **"My Beloved son Thorne"**, signed **"Your loving father, Ashe Blackwood"**. Full text in `../../drive/found-documents/a-letter-found-in-blood.md`.

Nobody at the table knows a Thorne. That is correct.

## Headcount

Let them do it themselves. **They come up one short, and the missing name is Sal's.** Unlike Thorne, Sal was not eaten: they remember him, his face, his orcs, his god. They do not remember him getting on the boat, or getting off it.

Then the evidence says two people are missing, not one. Let the players do that maths too.

Perception void on the pool, if Jack keeps the rule (`01-plan.md`): under 10, a pool of blood; 10 to 14, there was gear here and you cannot say what; 15+, someone was lying here and you cannot picture them.

**The sending stone.** Darla's stone is paired with Sal's. If she uses it, the message goes through. No answer. Silence, and breathing: slow, wet, like someone asleep or dying. Sal's stone is in Kael's things at the temple. See `05-sending-stone-and-goals.md`.

**No void stone on the beach.** Where Sal's necklace went is open.

## Three doors from the beach

**1. The farmhouse (Ashe Blackwood).** As the book: dogs, flintlock, DC 25 Persuasion or a fight, "I TOLD THAT DIRTY OLD MAN FAWKES I AIN'T LEAVIN" (Fawkes is a woman here; let Ashe get it wrong or fix the line, Jack's call). Show him the letter and he goes quiet. He wrote it, he does not remember writing it, and then he does. He asks which of them is Thorne. Nobody can answer. If the party is kind, Thorne's room and the house full of a family he cannot remember are the first real clues. Give the blade back and let that be a scene.

**2. The town.** Fawkes's re-election posters are the first thing they see: a woman in gold and silver armour, "TEN MORE YEARS". The Vanguard questions them at the edge of town. **Everyone is talking about Ser Kael Orendis**: the hero who died on the Reach two years ago walked out of the sea this morning, half-drowned and burning with fever. The mayor's own husband. Ilmater's miracle. The players know whose face is called Kael.

**3. The temple of Ilmater.** The sickroom. On the bed is Sal: his face, his scars, breathing badly. The sisters call him Ser Kael. If Darla uses the stone in the room, it speaks from the pile of his belongings. He does not wake. If it is evening, Fawkes is at the bedside and asks the strangers to leave. Do not explain anything. The temple's alms board has Q1 and Q5 on it.

The water is the door that is not a door. The hull will not float. Cid is in town and wants 5,000 gold or a week for the ferry.

## Handling the choices

- **Temple first.** The fastest punch. They find Sal and he is not Sal. Let them try everything: healing does nothing (a soul wound, Q1). Fawkes arriving that evening is the hook into town.
- **Farmhouse first.** Ashe is the gut punch, the town is the mystery, and the temple is the twist. Good order.
- **Town first.** Fine. The Kael gossip sends them to the temple, and Ashe becomes the mid-session blow.
- **Split the party.** Let them. The song that ends the session reunites them at the inn with no memory of walking there.
- **They attack Ashe.** He is old. If he dies, the letter is the only thing left of him. Do not soften it.
- **They try to wake Kael by force.** He does not wake for days without advanced healing. Fawkes and the sisters remove anyone who tries hard enough.
- **They decide the blood is Sal's.** Then the temple corrects them. Do not confirm it before.

## End of session 1

Whatever they are doing when it is time to stop: the song plays. Fade. They wake in the Dragon's Inn with cuts they do not remember and one thing in a pocket that was not there before (the cult plant: a necklace, a journal, or blue chalk on the door). Collect one memory card each. Next session opens there.
