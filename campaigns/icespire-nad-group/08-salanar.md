## The short version

**The Great Erasure and Liv's rampage are the same night.** Elara had a partner in her research, the island's healer, Brother Salanar. After Elara died, it was Salanar who put the spell in Liv's hands. Liv thought she was saving her father. Salanar thought he was ending the island's pain. Neither got what they wanted.

## Who Salanar was

- **The Vel'tharin are Lumenshire's healers**, an old order of Ilmater whose chapel stood on the small south-east island beside the courthouse. They sang **burden songs**: songs that lift pain out of the sick and carry it for them. That is why that island is now the Forgotten Reach.
- **Salanar** was their best healer and the island's: he set the bones and sat with the dying, and he probably helped deliver Liv.
- **Merith**, his partner, died slowly of a wasting sickness the songs could not lift.
- **Void stones** are shards of the portal Elara's ancestor opened. They wash up on the Reach. Held to a wound, they take the pain. Held long enough, they take the person. Salanar found them in his grief and kept going back.
- **Elara** was the only other person on the island who understood them. They became partners: she wanted the Forgotten's power and her family made whole; he wanted nobody on Lumenshire to ever hurt again. **The Oblivion ritual is their joint work.** Her book, his notes in the margins.

## The night (merging with the canon in `../../book/00-canon.md`)

1. Elara drowning Liv was not only cruelty. It was a preparation: only the portal-wizard's blood can open the void, and Liv is that blood. Fawkes walked in and killed her. (Salanar may have known what Elara was doing to the child. Jack's call how dark.)
2. Fawkes is tried for murder. Salanar goes to the grieving nine-year-old with Elara's book and a void stone. "There is a song that can make everyone forget what your father did. I'll help you."
3. In the courtroom Liv sings the spell. It is the Vel'tharin burden song, turned inside out. Salanar holds the stone: **the stone is meant to be the vessel**, the thing that holds everything the island forgets. Instead of just the trial, he means it to take the whole island's grief, Merith's death included. That is the Great Erasure.
4. Something goes wrong, maybe at Salanar's own hand: the stone cracks, he flinches, or he pulls it back at the last second because he cannot bear to hold it. With no vessel, the spell makes one out of the caster. **Liv becomes the hydra.** (This is the keeper clause from `../../book/05-sacrifice-and-hooks.md`, seen from the other side: the spell needed a keeper, and Salanar was supposed to provide one.)
5. Liv eats the courtroom, then the Reach. The Vel'tharin are unmade, and most of them become the Forgotten. **Liv's song is their burden song.** She sings it because she has them inside her.
6. **Kael Orendis**, Ashe's youngest and a young paladin of the island, fights through the chaos to the man who caused it. He confronts Salanar on the Reach as the bridge comes down. Salanar forces his soul into Kael.
7. **Salanar's empty body is eaten by Liv.** So the island has forgotten Salanar completely. No one remembers a healer by that name. Only Fawkes, who remembers everything, knows who helped his daughter cast that spell, and he believes that man is dead.

## After

- **Kael's body leaves the island.** Ashe remembers his son going: silent, wrong, one eye darker than it should be, and never writing back. That is in the letter.
- **Three years lost.** Salanar is not in control. He is drowning in Kael's life, living it from birth. The body is cared for at a shrine of Ilmater on the mainland by **Brother Aldric**.
- **Two years ago Sal wakes up**, Kael's whole life inside him and his conviction shattered. "Two years" on the sheet is two years awake, two years clean. He takes the name Sal and Kael's mission: **Lysa**, Kael's niece, taken by Calishite slavers from Lumenshire six months before that night. The slaver route is how Kael's body ended up in the south.
- **The last void stone on his neck is the one from the courtroom.** It is the vessel that failed, or that he refused to be. It is what Liv's spell was missing.
- **The two voices.** Ilmater, and the Unmaker. Candidates for the Unmaker: Elara, reaching him through the stone (fits Q2: she is the will inside the Forgotten); or **his own old self**, eaten and still inside Liv with everyone else, still convinced. Recommend leaving it ambiguous at the table and deciding for the finale.

## What this does to the campaign

- **The party already knows the words.** Sal is open about his past, so they have heard "Vel'tharin", "void stones", "the Great Erasure", "the Unmaker" as a goofball's sad story. On the island the words start showing up: a ruined Vel'tharin chapel on the Reach, margin notes in the Oblivion tome signed S., a burden-song hymnal in the church. They are standing in Sal's story and he is not there.
- **Liv's lair projections show the night.** A man in a healer's robe, his face a perception void (he was eaten), handing a little girl a black stone and saying "Ilmater, forgive me" the way Sal does at the table. That is the reveal, and the DM never says the name.
- **The sending stone.** When they say "Lumenshire" to Sal on the stone, the goofball goes quiet. Jack plays a Sal who knows exactly where they are and does not want to say. He still gives them the errand.
- **Ashe.** The letter is to the son who left the night of the attack. The man wearing that son's face is the one who caused it. Ashe does not know. Fawkes does not know Kael's body has Salanar in it. **In the future timeline, Sal walking onto the island in Kael's body is a bomb**: Ashe sees his son, Fawkes sees Kael, Liv knows the soul.
- **The keeper.** Salanar was supposed to be the vessel and refused. The keeper ending is his redemption made literal: in the future timeline, Sal can finally be what he would not be that night. And the stone on his neck may let the keeper live (the role the Vial of Complete Restoration plays in the book), or be the price.
