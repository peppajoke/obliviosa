# 01. Integration plan (rewritten 2026-10-13 to match `09-kael-returns.md`)

Current direction lives in `09-kael-returns.md` and `10-quest-board.md`. This file is the short version: how Lumenshire plugs into the Icespire game, and what this table changes from the book. If anything here disagrees with 09 or 10, they win.

## The host campaign

*Dragon of Icespire Peak*, run by **Nick**. Base is Phandalin. The dragon is Cryovain, a white dragon. The party met him once in a brief fight and got stomped, and is building up: contracts at the dwarf excavation (ancient ruins to the south-west), Gnomengarde, a horn that summons Charles the orc and his gang. Sal has spent the campaign befriending and rehabilitating orcs in Phandalin.

Lumenshire sits "far off the coast of Neverwinter" in every location doc. Phandalin is a few days from Neverwinter. Nothing moves.

## No dream frame

Decided 2026-10-13: the dream framing is gone at this table. Lumenshire happens after Cryovain, for real, in the same world. No past/future timelines, no waking up in Phandalin between sessions, no "it was only a dream". The book's `06-dream-frame.md` does not apply here.

## The handoff from Nick

Nick runs his campaign to its end. Nothing about his finale is guaranteed, and nothing here depends on how it goes. Brief for Nick: `03-handoff-for-other-dm.md`.

During the Cryovain epilogue in Phandalin, **Thorne Blackwood** finds Sal. Thorne is Ashe Blackwood's son and was Kael's old friend. He thinks Sal is Kael. He shows him his father's letter. Sal knows the trouble on Lumenshire is his fault, and he sails there with the party.

At the table, Nick plays one beat: a salt-stained stranger stops in front of Sal and says "Kael?" Cut to black. The players never see the rest of that conversation. They find out on the island.

## Entry

Jack opens on the book's cold open (`../../book/01-opening-the-letter.md`): the song in the dark, the beach, the wreck, the pool of blood, and the letter to Thorne from Ashe Blackwood. Session 1 is written in `04-session-1-intro.md`.

What happened between the cut and the beach, which the party does not remember:

1. Thorne, Sal and the party sail for Lumenshire.
2. On landing, **Liv eats Thorne** in the surf. Everyone forgets him, and with him the reason they came. The letter and the blood are what is left.
3. Liv sees her father. She recognises him and **tears Sal out of Kael's mind**.
4. **Kael** keeps the body, with his own memories up to the fight on the Reach two years ago. He is carried to the **temple of Ilmater**, unconscious and in critical condition.
5. Sal's soul goes back to the only other body it ever had: **Salanar's** corpse on the Forgotten Reach, two years dead. It rises as **Salanar Reborn**, a dark lich, and the campaign's boss.

So the party wakes one short. The missing name is Sal's. They remember him; they do not remember why they came.

Backstory, about two years ago: Kael and his party fought Salanar on the Reach. Salanar killed everyone but Kael. As Kael was about to kill him, Salanar jumped into Kael's body, and Kael still killed Salanar's real body. Sal relived Kael's whole life and came out dominant, changed by it. He went back to town believing his family erased, could not face the survivors, and left. Void crystals to forget, then a spirit bond with Ilmater and a paladin's vow to atone. That is the Sal the party knows.

## The fourth thread: putting Sal back together

Full detail in `09-kael-returns.md` and `10-quest-board.md`. The short version:

- **Kael** plays from Sal's sheet. His paladin powers do not work: his god will not answer half a man. He wakes after a few days (sooner if the party finds advanced healing, Q1 and Q2) and joins the Luminous Vanguard.
- **Separated, Kael and Salanar hate each other.** Persuading either to merge is DC 25 to 30, lowered by Liv, the temple, and the party itself as evidence of what Sal did with the merge.
- **Three merge methods:** Thaddeus Finn, missing, who can force it at a price (Q3); the Bound Hands of Ilmater, which needs both men willing (Q4); and Liv, who split them (proposal, Q5).
- **Ilmater wants them rejoined.** If the party stalls, he sends someone (proposal: Brother Aldric off the weekly ferry).
- **Sal's memories of the Cryovain campaign are locked until the merge.** Neither Kael nor the lich knows the party.

## Kit that matters

- **Revivify** (Rowan) works normally now that there is no dream rule. Diamonds are the limit; the party has one blue sapphire and no diamonds listed. It does nothing for a PC Liv eats: there is no body.
- **Four Rings of Warmth and Ice Knife on two casters.** The party is kitted for Cryovain. Lumenshire is not cold. Let them notice.
- **Lightning Bolt** (Rowan) is the Forgotten's weakness. **Blindness/Deafness** (Porridge) and Sprig's drum are song counters they already own.
- **Darla's sending stone** is paired with Sal's, and Sal's is now on Kael at the temple. See `05-sending-stone-and-goals.md`.
