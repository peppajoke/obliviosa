# For Nick: handing the party to Lumenshire

One page, DM-facing. Please keep it from the players; the opening only works if they do not see it coming.

**What it is.** After your Cryovain campaign ends, Jack runs a follow-on campaign on Lumenshire, an island far off the coast of Neverwinter. Same characters, same sheets, same inventory. It is real, not a dream. Jack DMs it, so Sal becomes an NPC there. How your finale goes is entirely yours; nothing here depends on it.

**What you do: one beat.** During the epilogue in Phandalin, whatever shape it takes, a salt-stained stranger walks up and stops in front of Sal. He looks at Sal's face like he knows it, and says:

> "Kael?"

Cut to black. That is the whole ask. No explanation, no follow-up scene. If Sal does not survive your finale, or the epilogue does not suit it, tell Jack and we will find another way in.

**Who the stranger is (for you, not them).** Thorne Blackwood, from Lumenshire. He knew the man whose body Sal wears. He has a letter from his father about trouble on the island, and Sal will realise the trouble is his fault. Off-screen, Sal agrees to go home with him and the party sails along. The players will not remember the voyage or why they went. That is the mystery Jack opens on.

**What happens to Sal.** He does not come back the same. On the island Sal and Kael are torn apart: the body wakes up as Kael, who has never met the party, and Sal's soul ends up somewhere much worse. Putting them back together is a big part of the campaign. It may not work.

**What you get back afterwards.** If the party leaves Lumenshire alive, they come back with whatever they earned there; Jack will send you a list so your sheets stay true. Sal's status depends on how they did:

- **Merged again:** Sal is back, with his memories of your campaign.
- **Not merged:** Sal is gone. Kael, in Sal's body, may stay on the island or come with them; still open.
- **Party lost:** Jack will tell you what that means for anyone you want to keep using.

None of this is guaranteed, and it is fine if your table has moved on by then.

**Small things you might notice.**
- The party has a lot of cold-weather kit. Lumenshire is not cold. No need to do anything.
- Sal's sending stone is paired with Darla's. If it matters in your game before the handoff, play it normally.

**What Jack needs from you.**
1. A yes to the "Kael?" beat and cut to black, and roughly when the epilogue will land.
2. The party's level and anything major they are carrying at that moment.
3. Whether Sal is alive and whole at the epilogue.
4. A yes to loot and XP carrying over both ways.
5. Whether you want the party back in your world afterwards, or are happy for Lumenshire to be where they end up.
