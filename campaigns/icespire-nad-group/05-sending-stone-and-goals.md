# 05. The sending stone and the goals on the island

Rewritten 2026-10-13 to match `09-kael-returns.md`. Was `05-goal-stones-and-origin.md`; the dream-era version (a live line to Sal in Phandalin, the errand to bring Ashe home, the origin wrinkle) is superseded.

## The sending stone

**Darla's stone is paired with Sal's.** Sal had his when he sailed. Now it is with the body: **on Kael, at the temple of Ilmater**, in the pile of his belongings by the bed. Accepted 2026-10-13 (09, "the sending stone reaching an unconscious Kael").

What Darla gets, by stage:

1. **Kael unconscious (the first few days).** The message goes through. Nobody answers. Silence, and breathing: slow, wet, like someone asleep or dying. Sometimes a murmur, never words. The players will hear Sal breathing and not know where he is. Let it sit.
2. **In the sickroom.** If Darla speaks into her stone while standing at the bed, her own voice comes out of his things. That is how the party can confirm the man on the bed is the one their stone reaches.
3. **Kael awake.** He answers once, hoarse and wary:
   > "Who is this?"

   He does not know Darla. He does not know he owns a sending stone, or why his god will not answer him. Every call after that, he is a stranger who is slowly getting curious, and then suspicious, about a halfling who keeps calling him by another man's name.
4. **After a merge.** Sal answers. He remembers them.

The stone is the party's thread to Sal, and it gives them nothing but evidence that he is alive somewhere. That is the point. The item allows one sending a day; the old draft's three a day was a dream-era ruling and is Jack's call.

**The stone does not reach the lich.** Salanar Reborn has Sal's soul but not Sal's stone. If a player asks, the stone is paired with an object, and the object is on Kael. Whether a clever party can get anything to the lich some other way is open.

## The goals on the island

Leaving the island is not a goal, it is a reward: no ship, no money, a weekly ferry, and Cid's price. The party has three goals, and they arrive in this order.

### 1. Get Sal back

The first goal the players form, before their characters understand it. They wake one short, and the missing name is Sal's.

How the goal sharpens:
- **Find him.** The gossip about Ser Kael Orendis, the temple, the breathing on the stone. They find his body and it is not him.
- **Learn what happened.** Kael wakes and does not know them. The graves keep getting opened (Q20). Something walks on the Reach (Q4). Signals that Sal and Kael were split, and that the other half is the thing on the Reach.
- **Learn what it costs.** Re-merging, not killing Liv, brings Sal back. Killing Salanar Reborn loses him for good. The players must learn that before the fight, not after.
- **Do it.** Three merge methods (Finn, the Bound Hands, Liv) and two men who hate each other (DC 25 to 30). Detail in `10-quest-board.md`.

Sal's memories of the Cryovain campaign are locked until the merge, so neither half knows the party. The party is the evidence of what Sal did with his second life.

### 2. Survive the song

Each time the song plays, every player rolls 2d6 damage and the DM takes one memory card each (`../../book/01-opening-the-letter.md`). Staying costs them who they are. The tools that block the song (`../../book/00-canon.md`) are worth chasing for their own sake, and several quests pay in them (Q2, Q16).

### 3. The festival clock

The **Remembrance Festival** is four days out. Every thread points at it.
- Fawkes needs the serum by then (Q6, Q3: Finn is making it under town hall).
- The cult calls it the Feast.
- Fawkes's offer of free passage home expires with it.
- The weekly ferry leaves the morning after.

The festival is where the threads converge whether the party chooses or not (Q9).

### The forgotten reason

The party also lost why they came. The letter in the blood is the only answer they have, and it is addressed to a man nobody remembers. Following it to Ashe is natural but not required. If they pick up the letter's ask (help Ashe, find out what happened to his family), it folds into the main mystery: what holds Ashe is the song, which means Liv, which means Fawkes.

## Open

- Whether Kael keeps the stone once he wakes, or Fawkes takes his things.
- Calls per day: one (the item) or three (the old ruling).
