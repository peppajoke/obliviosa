# 06. Retrofitting Lysa

**Superseded 2026-10-13.** The Lysa slaver story is dropped at this table (`09-kael-returns.md`). **Liv took Lysa's role:** the missing girl Sal's mission was about is Kael's daughter, and Sal's mission is atonement. None of the options below are current. Kept for the reasoning only. It also still assumes the dream frame, Ashe Orendis and Calimshan, all of which are gone.

Fixed by Sal's sheet: Lysa is Kael's niece, taken by Calishite slavers six months before the Great Erasure (about two and a half years ago). Finding her is Kael's mission and Sal's thread to redemption. Fixed by the integration: Kael is Ashe Orendis's son, born on Lumenshire, left young. So Lysa is Ashe's granddaughter, the child of one of the Orendis children Liv ate. Everything else about Kael is open.

Shared mechanism for most options: **the slaver ship wrecked on Lumenshire.** The raw notes already say travel to the island is obstructed and "there are MANY destroyed boats somewhere secret". Rowan's notes already give the waking game wreckers at a lighthouse, Mosco, who lure ships for Talos and are friends with Cryovain. A Calishite slaver making the northern run, lured onto the Reach, is one sentence and it ties both campaigns together.

## Options

**A. Lysa is alive on the island and nobody knows it, least of all her.** The slaver ship wrecked. Liv took the crew. Lysa, eight or nine, survived and walked into town, and the town absorbed her the way it absorbs everyone: nobody remembers her arriving, so she must have always been here. She is a nameless child in the residential district, or the "lost child near the cave" from the events list, or the kid who will not shut up about the election. The party can meet her a dozen times and never know. Payoff: Sal's whole mission ends on an island he refused to visit. Past ending: the party wakes remembering a girl and Sal gets his lead. Future: she is still there when they arrive for real. **Cost:** none to existing docs. **Recommended.**

**B. Lysa is Karleen.** Merge: the slaver ship wrecked, Lysa survived, Fawkes took her in and called her Karleen. Everyone, Fawkes included, remembers it as five years ago, because Liv edits time as easily as faces. Kael's sibling married an elf, so Lysa is half-elf; Karleen already is. Payoff: Sal's mission target is the campaign's key ally, the DON'T FORGET pages are being written by the girl the party does not know they came for, and she is the natural keeper. **Cost:** Karleen's age has to come down to late teens, and her backstory (family eaten in the rampage) becomes a false memory Fawkes gave her, which actually fits him. Biggest swing in the room.

**C. Lysa is already dead, and the island holds the answer.** The ship wrecked, Liv ate her with the crew, and her memories are inside the hydra with everyone else's. Ashe's house has a child's room that is newer than the others and he cannot say whose it is. Payoff: the keeper ending or killing Liv gives Ashe back a granddaughter he gets to grieve, and gives Sal an answer instead of a rescue. **Cost:** it closes Sal's mission with loss, which is a decision about Sal's arc, not just the island's.

**D. Lysa is with the cult.** The slavers who survived the wreck became the robed outsiders; Lysa grew up among them as Liv's favoured acolyte, half-turned, the author of the rewritten cultist journal. Payoff: the cult track gets a heart and a rescue, and the "she is a goddess who takes pain away" theory is spoken by a child who means it. **Cost:** she is in the darkest corner of the campaign, and the party may fight her before they know who she is.

## What is common to all four

- Ashe cannot remember Lysa. Her evidence is in his house. The party will find it before they know what it means.
- Sal, on the stone, will not say the name unless asked; he has never said it to anyone but Brother Aldric.
- The two campaigns share one missing girl, which means the Icespire DM should know which option Jack picks, because Lysa stops being a Calimshan thread the moment she is on the island.
