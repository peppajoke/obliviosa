# 10. The quest board

Decided (Jack, 2026-10-13): the table shifts to a **quest board model**. Offer the players quests, and each one leads to encounters. These are stubs: a pitch the players see, the truth the DM knows, the encounters, and where each quest leads.

## How the boards work

Four places post work. Each has a voice, and each feeds a different thread.

| Board | Voice | Feeds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Town hall notice board** | Official: Vanguard bounties, the mayor's errands | Fawkes track, the festival |
| **The Dragon's Inn board** | Locals: chores, odd jobs, lost things | The town's holes, the cult |
| **Temple of Ilmater alms board** | Healing, burial, night watch | Kael, the merge |
| **Pinned over the others** | Karleen's DON'T FORGET pages, blue chalk symbols | Karleen track, the cult |

Rules of thumb:
- Day one: post at least one quest on each board (the three-hooks rule in `../../book/05-sacrifice-and-hooks.md`).
- Quests the party ignores keep moving: a notice gets torn down, re-posted with a bigger reward, or replaced by news of what went wrong.
- Every quest pays something concrete (gold, an item, a favour) **and** reveals one piece of the truth.

## The merge: three ways to put Sal back together

Decided: there are several ways. Sal and Kael must merge again for our Sal to return, and separated they hate each other.

1. **Thaddeus Finn**, the island's mystic and the only wizard on Lumenshire powerful enough. He is missing (Q3). His way **can force the merge without consent**, but Finn's solutions always backfire, because the universe balances itself (canon). The price is the DM's choice, decided when they ask.
2. **The Bound Hands of Ilmater**, a relic (Q4). It works only if **both men grip it willingly**: the extremely high Persuasion checks. It is clean, and it is what Ilmater wants.
3. **Liv** (proposal). She split them, and she can put them back, if she trusts the party enough to give back what she took. This is the hardest and the most moving.

**Ilmater's thumb on the scale** (decided): Ilmater loves the merged Sal/Kael and wants them rejoined.
- **Kael's paladin powers no longer work.** He plays from Sal's sheet with no spells, Lay on Hands, Divine Smite or Channel Divinity. Martial only. He does not understand why, and it shames him in front of the Vanguard. In-fiction: his god will not answer half a man.
- **If the party is not making progress, Ilmater sends someone** (proposal: **Brother Aldric**, Sal's sponsor from his sheet). The escalation:
  1. The temple's priests dream of two hands bound with a cord.
  2. Kael, awake, kneels at the altar and gets nothing.
  3. Aldric steps off the weekly ferry. He was sent by a dream, and he has come for his friend. He knows Sal, not Kael, and he will not stop talking about Sal's atonement in front of a man who has never heard of it.
- **Persuasion helps** (accepted 2026-10-13): DC 25 to 30 for each man. Liv, the temple, and showing either man what Sal did with the merge (the party is the evidence) lower it or grant advantage. "Your god left you because you are not whole" is a true argument for Kael.
- **Killing Salanar Reborn loses Sal for good.** The players should learn that before the fight.

## Quests

### The temple (Kael and the merge)

**Q1. Remedies sought for Ser Kael Orendis** (temple board)
- *Pitch:* "The hero of the Reach has come home to us half-dead. The temple seeks any remedy, rare or costly. Ilmater bless the giver."
- *Truth:* a soul wound, not flesh. It needs Greater Restoration or better, which the party cannot cast.
- *Encounters:* the temple's sickroom; Fawkes at the bedside; the priests' rumours.
- *Leads to:* Q2 (the draught), Q3 (Finn could do it), Q5 (who visits at night).
- *Reward:* Kael wakes days early, before the Vanguard gets to him first; the temple's favour; free healing.

**Q2. Gary's short list** (the Dragon's Inn board, posted by Gary)
- *Pitch:* "Buying: one tide-pearl from the Reach shallows, three sprigs of grave-moss, a vial of banshee breath. Good coin, no questions."
- *Truth:* ingredients for a restorative draught that casts Greater Restoration once. The pearl means wading the Reach shallows.
- *Encounters:* Forgotten in the shallows (lightning works); grave-moss in the oversized graveyard at night (Q20); banshee breath from a Balm of the Banshee he only half remembers how to make.
- *Leads to:* Q1 (wake Kael), the song-resistance tools.
- *Reward:* the draught, and a Potion of Encapsulation at cost.

**Q3. Thaddeus Finn owes me 200 gold** (the Dragon's Inn board, posted by Willem)
- *Pitch:* "Anyone who finds Finn the mystic, tell him Willem wants his money. Shop's been shut a month."
- *Truth:* Fawkes is holding Finn under town hall, forcing him to make the festival serum (her keeper-by-force plan). He is the only wizard on the island who can force the merge.
- *Encounters:* the ransacked Mystic's Emporium (the book's missing clue: Finn's notes on "what the song takes, and how to give it back", and a Vanguard boot print); a Vanguard patrol; the secret lair under town hall.
- *Leads to:* the Fawkes track, the Karleen track, merge method 1.
- *Reward:* Finn, and a merge that will cost something.

**Q4. The fallen of the Reach** (temple board; Kael posts it himself once awake)
- *Pitch:* "Ser Kael Orendis asks for brave hands to bring his companions home from the Reach for burial."
- *Truth:* Kael's party died fighting Salanar two years ago. Their cleric carried **the Bound Hands of Ilmater** to bind Salanar. It is still on her body, near where the lich walks.
- *Encounters:* crossing to the Reach (no bridge: Thomas's boat in Q17, Cid, or swimming); the Forgotten; the bones of Kael's party; **the first sighting of Salanar Reborn**, who does not attack yet, because he recognises the relic.
- *Leads to:* merge method 2; Kael's trust; the lich thread.
- *Reward:* the Bound Hands, and Kael's oath of friendship (he does not know he has already given it).

**Q5. Night watch wanted** (temple board)
- *Pitch:* "The sisters seek a steady watch for the hero's sickroom after dark. Singing has been heard near the temple."
- *Truth:* Liv comes at night to look at her father.
- *Encounters:* the song very close; something enormous at the window; a three-headed silhouette that does not attack. Roll the song damage. Anyone who stays awake sees her gently touch the glass.
- *Leads to:* merge method 3; the realisation that the monster loves Kael.
- *Reward:* the truth, and the temple's trust.

### The mayor and the Vanguard (town hall board)

**Q6. Discreet couriers wanted**
- *Pitch:* "Collection at Lumen Pier, two days hence. Discretion rewarded. Apply at town hall."
- *Truth:* the book's potion pickup for Fawkes's serum. Another crew gets there first.
- *Encounters:* the pier at night; the rival crew; the marbles.
- *Leads to:* the Fawkes track and the festival.
- *Reward:* free passage home (Fawkes's offer), gold, the mayor's trust.

**Q7. A witness to be brought in**
- *Pitch:* "The Vanguard seeks help locating a person of interest. Marbles provided."
- *Truth:* the witness is Karleen, or someone carrying her drawings (the book's handoff from the Fawkes track to the Karleen track).
- *Leads to:* the Karleen track, or betraying her.

**Q8. Bounty: Forgotten sighted**
- *Pitch:* "Creature sighted by the graveyard wall. Fifty gold, a hundred if brought in whole."
- *Truth:* the Act 2 daylight Forgotten. Its body carries a clue pointing at Fawkes.
- *Encounters:* the Forgotten in the street, in front of everyone.
- *Leads to:* the Fawkes track; distrust of the Vanguard.

**Q9. Festival guards**
- *Pitch:* "Able bodies wanted for the Remembrance Festival. Pay and a place of honour."
- *Truth:* the clock. Guarding the festival puts the party next to the serum.
- *Leads to:* the finale.

**Q18. Escort duty, Blackwood farm**
- *Pitch:* "Assist the Vanguard in relocating a resident for his own safety."
- *Truth:* Fawkes is trying to get Ashe off the island alive. Ashe has a flintlock and two dogs.
- *Leads to:* Ashe; Thorne's empty room; the moral choice.

### Karleen and the cult (pinned over the others)

**Q10. DON'T FORGET**
- *Pitch:* a page pinned over a Vanguard notice, describing each of the party exactly, and a sixth person, crossed out. "Treehouse past the graveyard. Dusk."
- *Leads to:* the Karleen track.

**Q11. Missing: a child, last seen near the Reach shore** (the Dragon's Inn board)
- *Truth:* the cult's lost child, near the cave.
- *Leads to:* the cult track.

**Q12. Scrub the chalk** (town hall board)
- *Pitch:* "Two silver a wall to clean vandalism from the merchant district."
- *Truth:* the cult's blue chalk symbols. Followed in order, they lead to the cave.
- *Leads to:* the cult track.

### The town's holes (the Dragon's Inn board)

**Q13. Deliveries** (Bartholomew): "Need a runner for standing orders." Every address on his old receipts is an empty house with a table set for people nobody remembers.

**Q14. Who is in my portrait?** (Willem): a reward for anyone who can name the woman in the painting over his forge.

**Q15. A fitting** (Gwendolyn): "Seeking a woman of these measurements to try on a finished gown." No one on the island fits it.

**Q16. Hydra's Fury challenge** (Beatrice): "Drink three, win the house's thanks." The "so drunk the song cannot land" discovery. TJ's quest.

**Q17. Boat for hire past the Reach** (Thomas): the way across with no bridge, and the hidden boat graveyard.

**Q19. Parts wanted for a flying machine** (Cid): the escape valve.

**Q20. Gravedigger wanted** (the church): "The graves keep getting opened." It is **Salanar Reborn** taking the dead. The first lich thread, before the party knows what he is.

**Q21. A dream interpreter sought** (posted anonymously; Seraphina): her nightmare of a three-headed monster. A way to turn her.
