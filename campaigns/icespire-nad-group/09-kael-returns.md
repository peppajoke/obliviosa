## Decided (Jack, 2026-10-13)

**The handoff**
- Stay close to the original design: letter in blood, the forgotten friend, Ashe, the town.
- **Nick** is the Icespire DM. He hands off once Sal goes missing; nothing about his finale is guaranteed. He cuts to black during the Cryovain epilogue and Jack opens on the letter in blood. Sal is not with the party.
- **Thorne**, Kael's old friend, meets the party at the Phandalin epilogue. He thinks Sal is Kael and shows him his father's letter. Sal knows it is his fault and goes home with the party.
- On landing Liv eats Thorne, the party forgets why they came, and Sal reverts to Kael, who is welcomed home as a hero presumed dead. Kill Liv or lift the curse and Sal comes back.

**What happened on Lumenshire**
- **Liv is Kael's daughter.**
- **Salanar was unmaking Liv.** Her mother used magic to save her, and it **half worked**: Liv became the hydra.
- **Kael and his party fought Salanar.** Salanar killed everyone but Kael. As Kael was about to kill him, Salanar jumped into Kael's body. Kael still killed Salanar's real body.
- **Merged consciousness.** Sal relived Kael's whole life and emerged as the dominant personality, but changed a great deal, because he is also Kael now.
- **He left.** Kael-as-Sal went back to town believing his family had been erased. He could not face the survivors, and with his family gone there was no reason to stay, so he left Lumenshire. That is where Sal's atonement starts.
- **Becoming a paladin.** He took void crystals recreationally to forget. Then he formed a spirit bond with Ilmater, became a true paladin in Ilmater's light, and vowed to do everything he can to atone.
- **Sal/Kael replaces Fawkes as Liv's father.** Fawkes's other functions on the island need a new owner, because Kael left. That is resolved below.

## The fix (proposal): Liv's mother takes Fawkes's place

**The mother survived, and she is the mayor.** Every job Fawkes did on the island, she does, and better: a mother keeping her monstrous daughter alive.

| Fawkes in the book | The mother at this table |
| --- | --- |
| Liv's father, could not strike her | Liv's mother. Her spell made the hydra, half-saving her daughter. She will not let anyone finish it. |
| Hero of "the Forgotten's attack", elected mayor | Walked out of the Reach after that night as the woman who drove the monster back. Elected mayor. Founded the Luminous Vanguard under Seraphina. |
| Feeds Liv a captive every few nights | Same. |
| The only one who remembers everything | Her spell bound her to Liv. She hears her daughter. She is the only one who remembers. |
| Suicidal; the festival serum (keeper by force) | Same arc, with a mother's reason: pull the town's memories into herself and give her daughter back her body. |
| Took Karleen in, tried to move Ashe off his farm | Same. |
| Endings: his sacrifice, fleeing with his daughter, prison | Same, now hers. |

**Why Kael believed they were both gone.** After the fight he went back to the house: empty. Liv had been unmade as far as he knew, and his wife had vanished with the hydra into the Reach. He did not stay long enough to see her come back, and he could not face anyone who might ask. He took the boat. The island believes Kael died on the Reach with the rest of his party.

**What she believes.** That her husband died fighting the man who did this to their daughter. She has been a widow for two years.

**The return.** Two years later Kael walks out of the surf with no memory of anything after that fight. His wife is the mayor. His daughter is "gone". His wife knows exactly where their daughter is, and has been feeding the town to her. Whether she tells him, and when, drives the whole Fawkes track. She puts him on her posters: "Kael is home."

**Her name.** She takes Elara's slot: the portal-wizard's blood is why her spell could make a hydra at all. But this version of her is not cruel and not the villain, so she may want a new name. Keep "Elara" until Jack picks.

**The redux's Elara goal still fits, darker.** "Reunite her family into one being" no longer needs a ghost. With Kael home, her family is the hydra, Kael and herself.
