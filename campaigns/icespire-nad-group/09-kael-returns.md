## How the pieces fit (proposal)

### The split (decided)
When the party lands, Liv eats Thorne in the surf, and then she sees her father. **She recognises him and tears Sal out of his mind.** Kael keeps the body, with his own memories up to the fight on the Reach. Sal's soul goes back to the only other body it has ever had: Salanar's corpse on the Reach, where Kael killed it two years ago. It rises as **Salanar Reborn**, a dark lich. Our Sal exists only when Kael and Salanar are merged, and his memories of the Cryovain campaign are locked in that merge.

Proposal: what is in the lich is Salanar without the Cryovain years. He has Kael's life in him only as the thing that broke him, and the Unmaker voice is now the whole of him.

So **getting Sal back means getting two men who hate each other to merge**:

- **Kael** hates Salanar: the man who tried to erase his daughter, killed his party and stole two years of his life.
- **Salanar** hates Kael: the life that made him weak, and the man who killed his body.
- **Persuasion:** suggest DC 25 to 30 for each. Things that should give advantage or lower the DC:
  - Liv. Kael will do anything for her, and once merged, Sal loved her as his own.
  - Showing either of them what Sal did with the merge: his friends and the atonement. The party is the evidence.
  - Ilmater's temple. The ritual of re-merging plausibly happens there.
- **The cost of a fight.** If the party kills evil Salanar, Sal is gone for good. This is the campaign's sharpest choice, and the players should learn it before the fight, not after.

**Does this replace "kill Liv or lift the curse and Sal comes back"?** Yes, recommend it does. Lifting the curse restores everyone's memories, but Sal is not a memory: he is a merge. The two goals sit side by side: save the island, and put your friend back together.

### Kael at the temple
- **How he arrived:** found on the beach the morning after the wreck, or carried in by fishermen, half-drowned and burning with a fever that is the split itself. The town calls it a miracle: the hero who died on the Reach walked out of the sea.
- **The temple of Ilmater** is Sal's god's house. The priests are nursing their friend's body without knowing it.
- **Healing him early:** the injury is a soul wound, not flesh, so ordinary healing does nothing. It needs Greater Restoration or better, which a level 5 party cannot cast. The quest is for the means:
  - Gary's apothecary, or the Mystic's Emporium's remains.
  - A rare reagent from the Reach.
  - Or the **Vial of Complete Restoration** (already in the item list: "anchors one mind"), if Jack wants it used here rather than at the keeper ritual.
- **The sending stone:** if Kael still has Sal's stone, Darla's stone reaches an unconscious man. Silence, breathing, maybe a murmur. Once he wakes: "Who is this?"

### Fawkes, Kael's wife
- Her daughter is the hydra. She feeds the town to her. Her husband, dead two years, walks out of the sea. She is at his bedside every night and cannot tell him anything.
- When he wakes she puts him in the Vanguard, where she can keep him close and away from the truth.
- **She takes Elara's book roles too.** Her blood (the portal-wizard's line) is why her saving spell could make a hydra at all. That frees the book's Elara: **Elara is cut at this table.**
- **The will inside the Forgotten** (Elara in the book) becomes **evil Salanar**, if Jack wants the Forgotten to have a leader. The endings "kill Elara, save Liv" and "Elara wins" become Salanar endings.
- Her stat block: the book's Fawkes is a level 10 fighter. Either she stays a fighter who used a book she should not have, or she becomes a caster. Undecided.
