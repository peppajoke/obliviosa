## The reframe (proposal): how the Erasure made Liv without Sal knowing

The book's story stays intact: Elara's cruelty, Fawkes killing her, the trial, Liv casting Oblivion to save her father. **What changes is why it went wrong.** The Erasure tore the void open, and everything after it is fallout.

1. **Salanar** is Lumenshire's healer, one of the **Vel'tharin**, the island's small order of Ilmater. His partner **Merith** dies of a wasting sickness he cannot cure. He finds **void stones**, shards of the portal Elara's ancestor opened long ago, washing up on the Reach. They take pain away, and then people.
2. **The Great Erasure (about two years ago).** Salanar uses the stones to unmake the Vel'tharin, believing he is ending their suffering. Unmade people are forgotten, so the island does not remember the order existed. Only a hole remains. Unmaking that many people at once **tears the void open again**.
3. **Kael Orendis**, a young paladin of the island and Elara's younger brother, confronts Salanar that night. Salanar forces himself into Kael's body and **takes the next boat off the island**. As far as he knows, it is finished: his people are gone, the stones are spent, and he is running from what he did. The island believes Kael died that night.
4. **The open void reaches Elara.** She is the portal-wizard's descendant and already obsessed with the Forgotten, and now she can feel the door. Her cruelty to Liv becomes a purpose: Liv carries the same blood, so Liv can be the door. The drowning in the lake is that ritual. **Fawkes walks in and kills her.**
5. **The trial.** Liv casts Oblivion from her mother's books to save her father. With the void already open, the spell does not stop at the courtroom: the forgetting pours through her, and **she becomes the vessel**. That is the hydra. The rampage, the town forgetting and Fawkes's feedings all follow as in canon.
6. **Sal never knew.** He was on the mainland, drowning in Kael's memories, then waking up as Sal. He believed he had ended one terrible thing. He did not know he had started another.

**Why Salanar could take Kael's body:** Kael has the portal-wizard's blood too, the same blood that makes Liv a vessel. This is optional, but it means the swap and the hydra run on one rule.

**Timeline for this table:** the Erasure is about two years ago (Sal's "two years"); the trial is weeks or a few months after it. The book keeps its five years. Here the feedings have run for under two years.

## Arrival: why Sal reverts and the hook goes (proposal)

- **The hook:** by the canon rule, whoever Liv eats is forgotten by everyone, and anything that only made sense because of them goes too. The trip was Thorne's errand. Liv takes him in the surf, and the reason for the trip goes with him.
- **Sal:** the song takes what does not belong. Salanar is a soul living on a borrowed life, a graft of memories in someone else's head, and the song peels it off. Kael's own memories stay. Killing Liv or lifting the curse restores every lost memory on the island, and Sal comes back the way a memory card comes back.
- **Kael wakes in the surf** with his last memory being the night he confronted Salanar on the Reach. To him it is the same night. He walks into a town two years later, with his sister dead, his niece "gone", his brother-in-law as mayor, and a black stone on a cord he does not recognise.
- **Kael wants Salanar.** The man who did it is the first thing he will hunt. The party will know who he is hunting.
- Fawkes welcomes him home and puts him on the posters: "Kael Orendis is home. Ten more years." Fawkes knows everything. Whether he tells Kael about Liv is his choice, and the party's leverage.
