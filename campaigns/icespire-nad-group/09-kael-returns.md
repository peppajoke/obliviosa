# 09. Kael returns: the mayor is Sal with Sal taken out

Replaces `08-salanar.md`, which Jack rejected on 2026-10-13.

## Decided (Jack, 2026-10-13)

- **Fawkes is Kael Orendis.** At this table the mayor of Lumenshire is the man whose body Sal wears.
- **Liv is Lysa**, the girl Kael was trying to save.
- **It all happens in order.** No time travel. After the Cryovain fight Sal disappears. He ends up on Lumenshire, and the void stones, or Liv, wipe Sal out of Kael's mind, leaving Kael.
- **If the party kills Liv or lifts the curse, Sal comes back and rejoins the party.**
- **The players must understand that rule.** The campaign has to teach it to them.
- Keep it tight and light on characters.
- Unchanged: Kael, the body swap, and the broad strokes of Sal. The rest of the island story stays as written.

## The mechanism (proposal)

Canon already says anyone Liv eats is held inside her, and that killing her restores every lost memory. So **Liv ate Sal and left the body**. Kael's body walks around as Kael, and Sal's whole self is inside the hydra with everyone else she has taken. Lifting the curse or killing her lets him out. This keeps the rule simple enough for players to hold: *Sal is inside the monster.*

## Bridging Cryovain to the beach (options)

**A. The letter.** After Cryovain, a letter for Kael Orendis arrives in Phandalin from his father on Lumenshire. Sal says it is a personal errand and goes alone. He does not come back. The party follows the return address. Their ship wrecks on approach, the song plays, and they wake on the beach.

**B. The stone calls.** When Cryovain dies, Sal's last void stone starts humming, because void stones come from Lumenshire and something there has woken up. Over a few days Sal jokes less. Then one morning he is gone and his bedroll is scorched black. The party follows the stone's pull out to sea.

**C. Sal relapses to win.** At the worst moment of the Cryovain finale, Sal uses the void stone on the dragon to save his friends. It unmakes Cryovain and tears the void open, and Sal is pulled through to its other end, the old portal on Lumenshire. The party goes after him. This needs the Icespire DM's buy-in for their finale.

**D. The wreckers take him.** Mosco's Talos wreckers from the lighthouse (Rowan's notes, "friends with Cryovain") kidnap Sal for revenge and ship him north, where they lure ships onto the Forgotten Reach and its hidden boat graveyard. The party gives chase and is lured onto the same rocks. The Icespire DM can run the kidnapping.

**Recommended: A and B together.** The letter arrives and the stone starts humming in the same week. Sal leaves because he cannot tell which one is calling him home. No new characters, and the Icespire DM only has to play one scene in which Sal goes.

## Teaching the rule (use several, escalating)

- **The sending stone.** Darla's stone is paired with Sal's. On the island someone else answers it: "This is Mayor Kael. Who is this? How do you have this stone?"
- **The face.** The mayor is Sal, with no jokes, no warmth and no recognition. The posters are the first thing they see in town.
- **Bleed-through.** Each time the song plays, Kael has a moment of Sal: a joke, a party nickname, "guys?". Then it is gone.
- **Memory cards.** The first card the song takes from each player is one about Sal.
- **Liv's lair.** Her lair actions replay stolen memories. In this run they replay the party's own Icespire adventures, seen through Sal's eyes.
- **Sal's own message.** Before he went under, Sal knew and left something where they will find it, in the style of Karleen's DON'T FORGET pages: "It's me. She's got me. Kill the song."
