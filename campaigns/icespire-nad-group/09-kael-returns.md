## 1. The friend and the forgotten hook (proposal)

**The friend is Thorne Blackwood, unchanged from the book.** He is Ashe Blackwood's son, Lumenshire-born, and **Kael's oldest friend**. Kael and Thorne grew up together. When Kael left to be a paladin on the mainland, Thorne stayed. Two years ago word reached the island that Kael had died in the south fighting something monstrous (the Great Erasure, from the outside).

What happened, which the party has forgotten:

1. **Ashe's letter reaches Thorne:** "My beloved son Thorne... come home." People are vanishing, the house is full of strangers' things, and a song wails at night. Thorne sets out for the mainland to find help.
2. **He hears about the heroes who killed Cryovain**, and about one of them, a paladin with a face he knows. He finds them at the victory celebration in Phandalin and walks up to Sal: "Kael?"
3. **Sal, open about his past as ever, tells him the truth:** "I'm not Kael. I'm the thing that happened to him." Thorne takes it, barely, and asks anyway: "Then come as him. The island needs Kael. My father needs help. And you owe him a homecoming."
4. **The hook the party forgets:** dragon slayers are asked to sail to a dying island to kill a monster no one can remember, and Sal agrees to go home wearing a dead man's face, to pay some of his debt. The party went for their friend. Thorne paid for the boat. Sal knew what it would cost him to stand in front of Kael's people.
5. **They land.** Liv takes Thorne in the surf, and the song rolls over the beach. Everything else follows from that.

For Nick, this is one beat: **in the epilogue, a stranger in salt-stained clothes pushes through the celebration, stops in front of Sal and says "Kael?"** Cut to black. That is all Nick has to do. It works whatever shape his finale takes, and the last image the players have is that unexplained stranger.

## 2. Why eating Thorne reverts Sal and erases the hook (proposal)

**The party forgets the hook** by the canon rule: whoever Liv eats is erased from the memory of everyone who knew them, and anything that only makes sense because of that person goes with them. The trip was Thorne's errand. With Thorne gone, the voyage, the reason and the plan have no anchor, and they fall out too. The party wakes knowing only that they are somewhere they did not mean to be.

**Sal reverts** because of what Sal is: a soul living in borrowed memories. Salanar took Kael's body and lived Kael's whole life to get there. Two candidate rules, which can be combined:

- **The song takes what does not belong.** The song strips memory from everyone who hears it (the memory cards). Everyone else loses a few cards. Sal is nothing but cards, held in someone else's head. Salanar is the graft, and the song peels it off; the body's own memories, Kael's, are rooted and stay. What is left is Kael, remembering up to the moment he confronted Salanar two years ago. Then he is waking on the beach of his home island. *Restoration is the existing rule: killing Liv or lifting the curse restores every lost memory on the island, so Sal comes back the way a memory card comes back.*
- **Thorne was the thread.** Thorne is woven through Kael's whole childhood: every memory Salanar lived through to become Sal has Thorne in it. Liv rips Thorne out of all of them, and the weave Salanar was holding onto tears. The body falls back to its owner.

Recommend the first as the rule and the second as the reason it happened *this* night: Sal had survived songs before (he had never heard one), but this time the song came with Thorne's erasure tearing through the same memories.

**Kael's side.** Kael wakes in the surf with two years missing, strangers around him whom he does not know, and a black stone on a cord he does not recognise. He walks home. The town sees the hero it buried walk out of the sea. Fawkes and the Vanguard make him a symbol within days. Kael cannot explain how he got home, and that question is itself a thread for the party.

## How the party meets him

- **Posters.** Fawkes's re-election posters now carry the returned hero: Kael beside the mayor. "Kael Orendis is home. Ten more years." Sal's face, everywhere.
- **The stone.** If Kael wears Sal's sending stone, Darla's stone reaches it, and Kael answers: "Who is this?"
- **Meeting him.** Kael is a good man, a paladin, earnest, on Fawkes's side because Fawkes welcomed him home. He does not know them. When the song plays, maybe a flicker.
