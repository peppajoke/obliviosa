## How this table differs from the book

- No dream frame. Lumenshire happens for real, after Cryovain.
- The forgotten friend is the book's **Thorne Blackwood**, and the letter in the blood is the book's letter to Thorne from Ashe Blackwood. Thorne was Kael's old friend. He found Sal in Phandalin during the Cryovain epilogue, thought he was Kael, and brought him and the party home. Nick's only beat is "Kael?" and a cut to black.
- On landing Liv eats Thorne (the party forgets why they came), recognises her father, and tears Sal out of Kael. The party wakes one short: Sal.
- Liv is Kael's daughter; **Fawkes is a woman**, Kael's wife, Liv's mother and the mayor. Salanar, a healer on Lumenshire, was unmaking Liv, and her mother's magic half-saved her into the hydra. About two years ago, not five. Elara is cut.
- Kael lies unconscious at the temple of Ilmater, playing from Sal's sheet with his paladin powers dead. Sal's soul is in Salanar's corpse on the Reach, risen as the lich **Salanar Reborn**, the boss. Getting Sal back means re-merging them; killing the lich loses him.
- The table runs on quest boards (`10-quest-board.md`). Detail in `09-kael-returns.md`.
